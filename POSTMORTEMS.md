# Research postmortems

Concise, repository-wide lessons from closed research problems. These are
non-budgeted closure reviews, not a progress log or a substitute for a
project's PROGRAM.md. Every stopped or closed problem has one entry here before
the next problem is selected; the entry must change a future screen or method
choice, not merely summarize the work.

## 2026-08-05 — Open Conjecture Sweep / C74–C75

- **Stop trigger:** `OBSERVED` eligibility loss. Zhang, arXiv:2605.05243v1,
  Theorem 2, already proves the Holevo–Utkin conjecture for every
  \(d\ge4\), including the selected \(d=4\) case.
- **Failed check:** a title/author search of the originating paper did not
  search the exact conjecture statement, later arXiv records, or its citation
  trail; it missed a direct May 2026 solution.
- **Reusable rule:** before an open-problem attack, require an exact-statement
  search, an arXiv-ID/title search, and a current primary-source/citation
  check. A bounded null search is only `OBSERVED`, never eligibility proof.
- **Next-screen change:** C76 must document those three checks for every
  finalist before Oracle selection; known solved targets are excluded before
  any attack preregistration or code.

## 2026-08-05 — Open Conjecture Sweep / C76–C79

- **Stop trigger:** `PROVED` scoped closure. C79 independently certified the
  \(Q=I/2\) compatible endpoint; the re-audited C78 interpolation proves the
  arbitrary-qubit, three-qubit pair-support case.
- **Failed check:** C78 initially promoted a preprint proposition as a
  published theorem. Its mathematics survived, but its certification status
  did not, requiring an immutable withdrawal and reinstatement chain.
- **Reusable rule:** separate a proof's mathematical architecture from its
  admissible authority before sealing. A preprint may guide a reconstruction,
  but promotion requires either a published theorem with exactly checked
  hypotheses or a self-contained replayable proof.
- **Next-screen change:** for any candidate whose first bridge is a preprint,
  freeze an independent-source plan in the first proof preregistration and
  run the source-status check before, not after, paper drafting.

## 2026-08-05 — Open Conjecture Sweep / C80

- **Stop trigger:** `OBSERVED` compression-engine saturation. Exact controls
  passed, and all three frozen seeds produced a \(d=6,m=7\) compressed
  necessary-condition witness; none supplies a lift-forcing invariant.
- **Failed assumption:** a small compressed coordinate domain and a
  balance/zero-shift reduction would make the compatibility interface
  proof-producing. The latter still has \(1.59\times10^{14}\) pairs before
  nonzero PAF, while compressed PAF feasibility is easy.
- **Reusable rule:** distinguish a necessary compressed witness from a
  lift-compatible signature before estimating a compression engine's value.
- **Next-screen change:** require a stated exact lift-fiber invariant or
  quotient theorem before choosing compression as a primary attack; otherwise
  cap it at a convention control and one witness gate.

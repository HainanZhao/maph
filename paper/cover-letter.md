Please consider the enclosed manuscript, “Directional response jets of
suppressed multiphoton events in a four-mode Fourier interferometer,” for
publication as a Regular Article in Physical Review A.

Suppressed events are widely used to characterize multiphoton interference,
and prior work has established generic quadratic leakage under weak random
unitary disorder. Our manuscript resolves the coherent perturbation before
that directional information is averaged away. For a complete set of
calibrated output generators in a four-mode Fourier interferometer, we show
that specified dark events can remain identically zero or begin at quadratic
or quartic order. The cleanest near-term prediction is an exact
same-mode-pair quadrature contrast for four photons:

\[
P_{Y_{13}}(\epsilon)=0,\qquad
P_{X_{13}}(\epsilon)=\sin^2(2\epsilon)/16.
\]

The exact null holds at every angle and extends to an infinite odd-occupation
family. We also derive an \(F_4\)-specific, phase-resolved sectorwise
reciprocity from classical Krawtchouk duality. We use it as a structural tool
to place nominally noncyclic zeros in the closure of a cyclic selection law;
we do not present the classical Krawtchouk identity itself as new.

The manuscript explicitly distinguishes its contribution from earlier
random-disorder robustness estimates, Fourier-suppression diagnostics,
partial-indistinguishability benchmarks, and symmetric-power reciprocity.
It includes a finite-shot nuisance-floor calculation and releases exact
integer/rational verification code and tests as ancillary material.

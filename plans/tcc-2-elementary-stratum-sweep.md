## Objective and claim boundary

**Objective.** Mechanically establish the formal TCC for each *scanned*
admissible Appleby--Flammia--Kopp (AFK) tuple above dimension eight for
which the locked relevant one-place packet has complete quadratic support.
The proof route is:

```text
AFK convention theorem + exact support predicate
  -> proved Engine-A packet identification and trace descent
  -> exact characteristic/ray/multiplier bridge
  -> exact signed reconstruction and all rank-two minors
  -> both shifts and integral-form transport.
```

**Permitted terminal outcomes.**

1. `PROVED`: one replayable theorem row for each hit whose bridge and
   minor certificates close; or
2. `PROVED`: an exact *bounded* enumeration statement that the frozen
   scan universe contains no full-quadratic-support hit above dimension
   eight, conditional only on the Phase-0 theorem whose hypotheses are
   checked row by row.

**Not claimed.** This plan cannot prove TCC for an unscanned tuple,
partial or higher-order support, an arbitrary AFK modulus convention, or
an asymptotic disappearance theorem. An eligibility row is not a TCC
proof; a nonempty quadratic slice of a higher-order packet is reserved for
a different peeling project. Dimension six remains outside this program:
its analytic bridge has a recorded formulation gap.

**Stop condition.** Stop after (i) a negative G1 scan is sealed and its
bounded statement is handed to the census/outlook revision, or (ii) every
G1 hit has either obtained a Phase-3 theorem row or a preserved contained
failure/resource-cap record. Do not expand the universe or promote a
partial-support row without a versioned successor plan.

## Original objective and claim boundary

**Objective.** Determine, with a replayable theorem or a replayable
counterexample, whether AFK's sign-differenced order-ray partial-zeta
functional descends through the semisimple quotient of its finite
non-coprime flat/O_f-invertible ray-class monoid. Only if that descent is
proved, develop an Engine-A packet-identification theorem on the quotient
and then return to a separately preregistered form-aware TCC eligibility
scan.

**This project does not yet scan, identify an AFK packet, or prove TCC.**
The maximal-order group theorem, including the Effective-Stark
deleted-prime-cover result, may be used only after its hypotheses are
transported by a new label-preserving descent theorem. A numerical
agreement, a D12 bridge table, or a monoid-character expansion on the
semisimple quotient alone is not a descent theorem.

**Terminal outcomes.**

1. PROVED: a uniform AFK radical-annihilation/descent theorem with checked
   hypotheses; this authorizes a separately versioned quotient-Engine-A
   project.
2. PROVED: one admissible AFK order-ray counterexample for which the exact
   differenced functional is nonzero on the radical; this rules out the
   proposed universal ordinary-character Engine-A route.
3. CONTAINED: no conclusion after the frozen pilot; preserve every row and
   do not restart the original sweep.

The existing (Z/9Z,multiplication) partial-zeta construction is a
universal toy obstruction, not an AFK counterexample. It is evidence for
the first falsification gate, not an answer to this objective.

## Frozen first pilot and gates

### P1 — exact AFK monoid/function adapter

**State:** PREREGISTERED; source-interface audit active.

Before evaluating any target functional, freeze:

- AFK source hash/version, exact equation defining the squared overlap,
  and the sign class/conventions record;
- one fixed nonmaximal admissible tuple/form row selected from the existing
  d<=1024 skeleton by canonical tuple ID and form data, together with a D4
  conductor-one calibration;
- an exact finite presentation of M, basis ordering, multiplication table,
  and the map from AFK ideals/forms to M;
- the exact functional to be tested, including any analytic regularization
  and the declared closure criterion; and
- PARI/Python versions, resource cap, JSON schema, hashes, and the rule
  that every failed row is retained.

**G1 acceptance.** The adapter must reproduce the D4 group control and
independently reproduce the chosen AFK squared-overlap labels. Failure
halts P2; no quotient computation is interpreted.

**Contained shortcut.** Kopp's monoid-resolution proposition gives a
surjective exact sequence from (O/m,multiplication) times the sign monoid
over the ordinary ideal-class monoid. Its definition of monoid exactness
does not make the left map injective. Consequently this project must not
replace the AFK monoid by a residue-ring monoid, or by residue classes
modulo guessed unit orbits, until the equivalence relation has been
derived and checked.

### P2 — radical-annihilation pilot

**State:** BLOCKED on G1.

Compute the radical J subset Q[M] exactly by rational linear algebra and
construct an explicit basis. Evaluate the pinned AFK differenced functional
on that basis by an exact formula or a preregistered rigorous enclosure.

- A certified nonzero value (enclosure excluding zero by its recorded
  margin) closes terminal outcome 2 for the universal ordinary-character
  route.
- Vanishing on every basis vector is only a CERTIFIED_NUMERICAL or
  finite-row result. It authorizes P3, not a universal claim.

### P3 — uniform descent theorem

**State:** BLOCKED on a P2 all-zero pilot.

Prove a structural identity forcing ell(J)=0, including local non-coprime
primes, or precisely characterize the surviving quotient. The proof must
preserve AFK's labels and the sign difference. A reduction that loses the
representative needed by the characteristic bridge fails this gate.

### P4 — quotient Engine-A closure

**State:** BLOCKED on G3.

Only now import maximal-order mechanisms: primitive characters,
imprimitive Euler factors (including the deleted-prime cover criterion),
quadratic support, Tate-compatible regulator/unit closure, and Fourier
reconstruction. Each transfer hypothesis must be checked at the individual
order-ray stratum.

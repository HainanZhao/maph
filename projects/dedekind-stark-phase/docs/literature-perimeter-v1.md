# Primary-source perimeter

Frozen: 2026-07-30 UTC.

1. X.-F. Roblot, *Index formulae for Stark units and their solutions*,
   arXiv:1112.2820, especially the unconditional quadratic, quartic,
   and conditional sextic weak solutions. The paper describes the
   conclusion as a weak Stark statement “up to absolute values”:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.2820>.
2. T. Shintani, *On certain ray class invariants of real quadratic
   fields*, J. Math. Soc. Japan 30 (1978), 139--167:
   <https://doi.org/10.2969/jmsj/03010139>.
3. S. Yamamoto, *On Shintani's ray class invariant for totally real
   number fields*, arXiv:0805.4282. The paper proves the
   place-factorization and sign-change relations using multiple
   sine/cone machinery:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.4282>.
4. Z.-Y. Kong and L.-P. Teo, *An elementary proof of the
   transformation formula for the Dedekind eta function*,
   arXiv:2302.03280:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03280>.
5. C. O'Sullivan, *Integrality of the higher Rademacher symbols*,
   arXiv:2306.08831. Its introduction records the classical link
   between the Rademacher symbol, Dedekind sums, and real-quadratic
   narrow-class zeta values:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08831>.
6. E. Tsukerman, *Fourier--Dedekind sums and an extension of
   Rademacher reciprocity*, arXiv:1307.5542:
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.5542>.

## Initial literature finding

The primary sources support two adjacent facts:

- Dedekind/Rademacher sums govern modular multipliers and certain
  real-quadratic zeta values.
- Shintani--Yamamoto invariants are built from cone decompositions and
  multiple sine functions.

They do **not**, in the inspected statements, supply a formula for the
quartic rank-one Stark phase defect. The proposed bridge remains a
conjectural research question. The absence claim is bounded to the
sources above and is not universal.

# Cycle 11 decision: embedding robustness and its boundary

`PROVED`: The separator theorem is robust under filtration-preserving surface
homeomorphisms, coboundary gauge changes, completion-chain changes,
transported affine quadratic corrections, and invertible coordinate changes
supported wholly on one side of each declared cut.

`PROVED`: Stabilizing the surface away from the graph appends a rank-one
four-state factor and leaves the normalized Arf sum unchanged.  Such a
gauge-redundant stabilization is noncellular.  In a connected cellular
embedding, the graph one-skeleton surjects onto surface homology, so no
surface handle can be homologically unused.

`PROVED`: The complete pre-Arf family is not invariant under arbitrary
rotation changes.  The same `K_(3,3)` graph has explicit genus-one and
genus-two cellular rotations, producing 4 and 16 spin structures.  No
invertible equivalence exists within a common state space of the stated
dimensions.

`CERTIFIED_NUMERICAL`: Exact face, homology, tree-cotree, and finite-field
checks over two primes verify both rotations and show that their normalized
Arf contractions equal the same directly enumerated even-subgraph value.

Together with the infinite K3,3-chain H3 obstruction, this completes Upgrade
4: the positive robustness class is explicit, and the first failed condition
outside it is identified.  No claim of arbitrary embedding independence is
made.

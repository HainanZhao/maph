\section{Noncanonical internal rank}

Before canonical normalization, a raw coordinate tensor at
\(G_{10,3}\) has apparent central internal rank \(512\).  Transporting the
spin labels, Walsh phases, quadratic correction, and minor indices through
the full symplectic change gives canonical ranks \(256,256,256\) across the
adjacent pair/internal/pair cuts.  The raw excess is a coordinate artifact.

Earlier intermediate factorizations gave only
\(R_{\rm pair}\le512\) and \(R_{\rm internal}\le1024\).  These were
pre-quotient bounds.  They are not saturation certificates and cannot be
used in place of the canonical rank profile.

\section{Invalid reflected encoder}

Reflecting the normal five-layer encoder does not produce a valid right
encoder in the same global canonical labelling.  At widths \(4,5,6\), its
exact right ranks are \(7,12,15\), against targets \(15,24,35\), while its
left ranks are full.  The false step was to assume that two independently
chosen side completions could be absorbed by one mask diagonal.  One global
cochain gauge need not trivialize both completions.  Any generic-tightness
proof must use the independently defined opposite-phase encoder or a
different right-observability mechanism.

\section{Current manuscript consequence}

The main manuscript therefore uses only canonical coordinates, separates
the normal and opposite encoder constructions, and marks the
arbitrary-width encoder lemma proof-incomplete until the full contracted
dual quotient edge sets and width-four bases are printed.

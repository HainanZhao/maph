\section{Revision status and dependency ledger}

The headline grid upper bound and generic-saturation statements now have
self-contained arbitrary-width arguments.  Exact finite-field computations
are audit firewalls, not substitutes for those arguments.  The direct
local-core replay and the denominator-free global phase telescoping check are
complete.

The first public version contained a character-duality error: its
internal-cut H3 step used the longitude cocycle
\(\operatorname{PD}(b_i)\), which extracts the \(a_i\)-coordinate, where the
\(\lambda_{b_i}\) character requires the meridian cocycle
\(\operatorname{PD}(a_i)\), which extracts the \(b_i\)-coordinate.  The
corrected proof treats H1 for the exposed longitude and H3 for the pushed-off
meridian separately.  Under the triangular canonical-basis correction,
\(\operatorname{PD}(a_i)\) is unchanged.

A statement is promoted only when its own written proof and every named
dependency are complete; finite-field agreement never promotes it.  The
nontrivial dependency graph is
\[
 \text{canonical basis}\to\text{grid H1--H3}\to
 \text{polynomial grid TT}\to\text{generic minimality},
\]
\[
 \text{encoder lemma}\to\text{reachability/observability}\to
 \text{generic minimality},
 \qquad
 \text{separator theorem, phase telescoping}\to\text{polynomial grid TT}.
\]
The genus calculation is parallel rather than a premise of compression.  The
\(K_{3,3}\) obstruction and the Walsh-marginal contraction are independent
of the generic-lower-bound branch.

\section{Noncanonical internal rank}

Before canonical normalization, a raw coordinate tensor at
\(G_{10,3}\) has apparent central internal rank \(512\).  Transporting the
spin labels, Walsh phases, quadratic correction, and minor indices through
the full symplectic change gives canonical ranks \(256,256,256\) across the
adjacent pair/internal/pair cuts.  The raw excess is a coordinate artifact.

Earlier intermediate factorizations gave only
\(R_{\rm pair}\le512\) and \(R_{\rm internal}\le1024\).  These were
pre-quotient bounds.  They are not saturation certificates and cannot be
used in place of the canonical rank profile.

\section{Invalid reflected encoder}

Reflecting the normal five-layer encoder does not produce a valid right
encoder in the same global canonical labelling.  At widths \(4,5,6\), its
exact right ranks are \(7,12,15\), against targets \(15,24,35\), while its
left ranks are full.  The false step was to assume that two independently
chosen side completions could be absorbed by one mask diagonal.  One global
cochain gauge need not trivialize both completions.  Any generic-tightness
proof must use the independently defined opposite-phase encoder or a
different right-observability mechanism.

\section{Current manuscript consequence}

The main manuscript therefore uses only canonical coordinates, separates
the normal and opposite encoder constructions, prints the full contracted
dual quotient incidence data and width-four bases, and treats the exposed
longitude H1 and pushed-off meridian H3 obligations independently.  The
superseded longitude-H3 step remains withdrawn.

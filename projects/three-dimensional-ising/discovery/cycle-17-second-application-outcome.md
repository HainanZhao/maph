# Cycle 17 outcome: optional second application stopped

`PROVED` topological prefilter:

- A finite honeycomb or triangular strip with free transverse and
  longitudinal boundaries has its natural cellular embedding in a disk, so
  `g=0`; the complete pre-Arf family is one scalar.
- Making only the transverse direction periodic gives a cellular embedding in
  a cylinder, still genus zero after capping boundary components.
- Making both directions periodic gives the natural torus embedding, whose
  genus is one independent of strip length.  It supplies only one handle site
  and no longitudinal sequence of nontrivial pair cuts on which separator
  compression could be tested.

`OBSERVED/DECISION`: these natural non-grid candidates fail the preregistered
nontriviality gate before H1--H3 become informative.  Producing genus growing
with length would require an engineered nonminimum rotation system, explicit
co-cores, and an internal H3 proof of the same kind and scale as the cubic
checkerboard construction.  The stop rule therefore fires.  No second
application is added to the manuscript, and the question remains open.

This is not a no-go theorem for honeycomb or triangular embeddings; it is the
terminal outcome of the optional bounded probe.

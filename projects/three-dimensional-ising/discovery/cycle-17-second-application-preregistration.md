# Cycle 17 preregistration: optional second application

Date: 2026-08-08  
Budget ordinal: B17

## Decision question

Does a natural honeycomb or triangular-lattice strip give a nontrivial second
positive instance of the abstract separator theorem without requiring a new
embedding proof comparable to the cubic-grid argument?

## Acceptance

A non-grid strip family with nonconstant spin-structure data, an explicit
surface filtration, relative-chain spaces, attainable trace dimension, and
written H1--H3 verification.  Genus-zero scalar families and fixed-genus
examples with no nontrivial handle cut do not count.

## Stop rule

Inspect the natural planar cylinder and toroidal closures first.  If they are
topologically trivial for the all-spin-structure question, and obtaining
growing genus requires an engineered rotation/co-core proof, stop.  Do not
start a checkerboard-scale embedding construction; T7 must not delay the
paper.

## Falsifier

Any character whose restriction to a future relative-cycle space is not a
coboundary plus separator trace, or any internal phase not determined by the
frontier trace, rejects that embedding.  Absence of a nontrivial handle cut
rejects the candidate as an application even if H1--H3 hold vacuously.

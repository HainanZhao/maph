# Cycle 10: abstract separator theorem

## Decision question

Can the checkerboard compression proof be stated as an intrinsic separator
criterion with a genuinely non-grid, unbounded-genus application?

## Question the questioning

A theorem that merely renames grid coordinates is excluded.  The hypotheses
must live on relative chain spaces and interface traces, and the non-grid
family must expose whether the internal-handle condition is substantive.

## Exclusion map and selected mechanism

- Grid-only co-core language is replaced by relative chain spaces `P_L,P_R`,
  trace maps `tau_L,tau_R`, and phase factorization through their common
  attainable mask module.
- A first claim that toroidal `K3,3` two-sum chains had binary bond two was
  falsified: middle internal cuts have exact rank four.
- Selected corrected theorem: H1--H2 imply the separator bound at pair cuts;
  H3 is precisely the additional condition retaining that bound inside a
  handle.  Without H3, splitting a four-state core gives a factor-two bound.
- Non-grid application: oriented edge-two-sum chains of toroidal `K3,3`
  gadgets, with handle-site bond two and binary bond four.

## Input, verifier, stop, and falsifier

- Input: the Cycle 7 coefficientwise gluing identity.
- Invariant: the attainable even-mask module on the separator.
- Smallest verifier: exact topology and all-q tensors for one through four
  `K3,3` gadgets over two primes.
- Falsifier: pair rank above two, failure of genus additivity in the explicit
  rotation, disagreement of independent cup/tree-cotree intersections, or an
  internal rank-two claim surviving a rank-four minor.
- Stop: four gadgets and two primes are validation only; the arbitrary-length
  statement must follow from the algebraic separator proof.

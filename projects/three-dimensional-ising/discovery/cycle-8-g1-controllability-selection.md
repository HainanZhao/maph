# Cycle 8: G1 generic-minimality campaign

## Decision question

Is the `d_w=2^(w^2-1)` checkerboard all-spin-structure realization minimal
over the field of rational functions in independent edge weights for every
width `w`?

## Question the questioning

A full `d_w` minor is the conclusion, not a viable first computation.  The
right invariant is minimality of a weighted automaton: reachability from the
left boundary and observability at the right boundary.  A special edge-weight
assignment proves generic minimality only if it preserves the canonical
spin-structure inputs and supplies a replayable nonzero minor.

The first proposed deterministic assignment was a pair of separator-spanning
trees whose unique T-joins have injective homology labels.  This is sufficient
but not necessary; failure of that specialization cannot be promoted to rank
deficiency of the full tensor.

## Exclusion map

- Former question: does genus cause auxiliary rank beyond `V_w`?  Cycle 7
  proves no for the checkerboard embedding (`G0`).
- Former state: a transverse-slice potential map should itself be surjective.
  Exact audits disprove this; at `w=3` its rank is at most three, while the
  complete tensor still reaches rank 256.
- Former specialization: rectangular longitudinal detours for the edges of a
  separator tree.  The common graphic/linear rank plateaus at 6 rather than 8
  for `w=3`, 11 rather than 15 for `w=4`, 22 rather than 24 for `w=5`, and 31
  rather than 35 for `w=6`.  Added depth does not repair the missing classes.
- New delta: use the algebra generated by canonical `q`-conditioned transfer
  cores, not a single deterministic T-join per frontier mask.

## Brainstormed mechanisms

1. **Selected:** prove reachability and observability through the finite Weyl
   algebra on `V_w`.  Independent transverse weights give a simple-spectrum
   diagonal operator; longitudinal weights give a dense convolution.  It
   remains to prove that the canonical handle alphabet exposes enough of this
   algebra rather than only a proper invariant subalgebra.
2. A unique-extremal-monomial determinant built from a homology-injective
   separator tree.  Retained as a sufficient criterion but excluded as the
   primary grid proof by the exact plateau above.
3. Induct on `w` by a block decomposition of the projected minor.  Promising
   only after the carrier algebra is identified; otherwise the block signs
   recreate the canonical-coordinate problem.

## Frozen verifier and stop

- Input: Cycle 7 canonical TT cores, checkerboard co-core ordering, and
  independent edge-weight variables.
- Map: the associative algebra generated by the four conditioned matrices of
  each handle type, restricted to the global-spin-flip even sector.
- Smallest verifier: `w=3` must regenerate all `256^2` matrix units or prove
  equivalent full commutant dimension without enumerating `4^g` sectors.
- Advance condition: a width-symbolic proof that the generated algebra is
  `End(V_w)`, followed by a finite word-length reachability bound `n_0(w)`.
- Resource stop: sparse generators only; no dense `d_4 x d_4` matrices, less
  than 8 GiB and 30 minutes per exact run.
- Falsifier: a non-scalar operator commuting with every canonical conditioned
  core for an infinite width family, or a proved invariant subspace containing
  the boundary vector.

## Claim boundary

G1 is not proved by finite widths, by the full physical transfer algebra
alone, or by genericity language without one exact specialization.  Any
successful proof leaves the carrier area-exponential for cubic boxes.

## Operational definitions and outcomes

An **operational bridge** has two independently checkable parts:

1. an interface theorem defining finite-characteristic selection, the
   additive-to-logarithmic operation, branch continuation, boundary finite
   part, and identification with the AFK cocycle/ray classes; and
2. a fusion-continuity theorem for that defined object along the attracting
   `A_6` geodesic, preserving the norm-37 label.

A **Class-A reduction** is a proof-grade, replayable reduction of the
operational bridge to a theorem on an explicitly compact or finitely covered
parameter set with pinned conventions and a strict exact or
`CERTIFIED_NUMERICAL` margin. New notation, floating-point recognition,
interface-free estimates, and unproved compactness are not Class A.

Terminal outcomes are:

- `A_CLOSED`: the interface and fusion theorem are proved and exact downstream
  replay closes dimension-six TCC;
- `B_REDUCED`: a Class-A reduction is proved, but its terminal inequality or
  finite certificate remains open;
- `C_FROZEN`: dimension six is not proved by `C254/B091`; any surviving
  reduction and exact recovery point are handed off, and the project stops.

## Definitions used by the campaign

An **operational bridge** consists of two independently checkable parts:

1. an interface theorem defining finite-characteristic selection, the
   additive-to-logarithmic operation, branch continuation, boundary finite
   part, and identification with the AFK cocycle/ray classes; and
2. a fusion-continuity theorem for that defined object along the attracting
   `A_6` geodesic, preserving the norm-37 label.

A **Class-A reduction** is a proof-grade, replayable reduction of the
operational bridge to a theorem on an explicitly compact/finitely covered
parameter set with pinned conventions and a strict exact or certified-
numerical margin. A heuristic reformulation, floating-point pattern, new
notation, or inequality lacking the interface is not Class A.

Campaign outcomes are:

- `A_CLOSED`: both interface and fusion continuity are proved, and exact
  downstream replay closes dimension-six TCC;
- `B_REDUCED`: a Class-A reduction is proved but its terminal inequality or
  finite certificate remains open;
- `C_FROZEN`: no Class-A reduction survives 100 campaign cycles, so dimension
  six freezes and the primary effort moves to pattern mining.

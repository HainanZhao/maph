# SIC--Stark research cycle 124: stabilizer action on the blocks

Date: 2026-07-28

Let

\[
 B_c=\begin{pmatrix}4&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 A=\begin{pmatrix}115&-24\\24&-5\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then

\[
 B_c^{-1}AB_c=
 \begin{pmatrix}115&-6\\96&-5\end{pmatrix}
 \equiv I\pmod6.
\]

So the real-multiplication stabilizer normalizes the coefficient
Heisenberg lattice and acts trivially on its level-six quotient labels.
On the central character it sends

\[
 W_r\longmapsto W_{-r}.
\]

This is exactly the block pairing required by the reflection
\((\mu,h)\mapsto(Q-\mu,-h)\). The fixed blocks are \(W_0,W_2\);
\(W_1,W_3\) are exchanged.

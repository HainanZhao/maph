## The lower field is the ray-\(12\) field

The four nontrivial lower-conductor values are the real roots of

\[
P_{\mathrm{low}}(X)
=X^8-8X^7+12X^6+8X^5-22X^4
 +8X^3+12X^2-8X+1.
\]

An exact class-field computation identifies this field with the
one-place ray class field

\[
H_{12}/K,\qquad
K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt5),\qquad
\mathfrak m=(12)\infty_1.
\]

The one-place and both-place ray groups are

\[
C_2^2,\qquad C_2^3.
\]

Base conjugation and the forgetful map are

\[
\iota=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&1&1\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
F=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&1\\
0&1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Consequently the commutator subgroup is generated by \((0,1,0)\),
whereas the kernel of \(F\) is generated by \((1,0,1)\).  They are
distinct order-two subgroups, proving

\[
[H_{12}:H_{12}\cap\mathbf Q^{\mathrm{ab}}]=2.
\]

This is precisely the Shintani index-two situation that made dimensions
five and seven unconditional.

## Exact Shintani denominator

## Rigorous analytic identification

The dimension-five and dimension-seven Arb integrator has been extended
to

\[
\beta=\frac{7+3\sqrt5}{2}.
\]

The near-zero proof now uses the rational hypotheses

\[
\beta<7,\qquad |\beta+1-2z|<8,

at \(60\), where its remainder is bounded by \(e^{-60}/16\).

\[

\[
1.34\cdot10^{-10}.

\[
7.68\cdot10^{-8}.

The height gap proves the four root identities.  Zauner symmetry and
reciprocity supply all fifteen lower-conductor characteristics.

## Primitive analytic windows

The same Arb proof has been run on eight reciprocal representatives of
the primitive packet.  Every analytic squared overlap meets its isolated

\[

The primitive Fourier packet has four unconditional quadratic
components and two conjugate cyclic-quartic pairs.  In the notation of

\[

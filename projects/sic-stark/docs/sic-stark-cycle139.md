# SIC--Stark research cycle 139: AFK wrap holonomy

Date: 2026-07-28

AFK quasiperiodicity gives

\[
 \nu(a,b+6)=(-1)^a\nu(a,b).
\]

Thus the primitive quotient satisfies

\[
 R(a,b+6)=-R(a,b).
\]

All \(24\) primitive directions are symplectic transports of this
calculation.  The result fixes the line-bundle convention exactly.
It must, however, be interpreted on the correct side of Fourier
duality.

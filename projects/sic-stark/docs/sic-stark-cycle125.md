# SIC--Stark research cycle 125: comparison of the two polarizations

Date: 2026-07-28

The coefficient and ideal lattices both have index four, but their Smith
forms differ:

\[
 B_c=\operatorname{diag}(4,1),\quad \operatorname{SNF}(B_c)=(1,4),
\]

\[
 B_i=2I,\quad \operatorname{SNF}(B_i)=(2,2).
\]

They are therefore not related by an integral unimodular change of
polarization. Their intersection has index \(8\), and their sum has
index \(2\).

The relation between their six-dimensional blocks is nevertheless
explicit. Translation by \(12\) pairs

\[
 |r+4\ell\rangle
 \quad\text{with}\quad
 |r+4(\ell+3)\rangle.
\]

Each \((U^{12},P^{12})\) ideal block is the direct sum of a
three-dimensional \(P^{12}\)-eigenspace in \(W_r\) and the corresponding
eigenspace in \(W_{r+2}\). Thus the required polarization change is a
two-point Fourier transform inside each coefficient block, followed by
a \(3+3\) gluing.

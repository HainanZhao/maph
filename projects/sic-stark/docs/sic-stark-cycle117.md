# SIC--Stark research cycle 117: the Fermat-boundary gate

Date: 2026-07-28

## Exact singular sector

For every rational step, the nonzero characteristics with a vanishing
cyclic factor are exactly

\[
 bm-an\equiv0\pmod6.
\]

There are five of them; together with the exceptional zero
characteristic they make the complete central sector \(z^n=1\).

In the reciprocal \(d_n^*\) normalization, the associated Fermat
parameter contains a factor \((1-z^n)^{1/n}\).  Hence this sector lies on
the singular Fermat boundary, outside the punctured parameter locus of
the ordinary pentagon theorem.

The numerator and denominator \(q\)-gamma parameters agree, so their
ratio is finite.  But the individual cyclic operators are singular
before the ratio is formed.  A valid operator proof must therefore use a
renormalized pentagon or transfer identity for the ratio; applying the
ordinary pentagon term by term is not justified.

This identifies precisely where the cycle-63 \(q\)-gamma correction
enters the operator problem.

# SIC--Stark research cycle 126: the restricted inversion Gaussian

Date: 2026-07-28

Ishibashi's even-level inversion relation at \(N=24\) contains

\[
 \gamma_{24}(h)=\zeta_{48}^{h(h-24)}.
\]

On \(h=r+4\ell\),

\[
 \gamma_{24}(r+4\ell)
 =
 \zeta_{48}^{r(r-24)+8r\ell}\gamma_6(\ell)^4.
\]

By itself this is a degenerate fourth power of the level-six Gaussian,
not the nondegenerate metaplectic chirp required by the AFK Weyl kernel.
Moreover,

\[
 \frac{\gamma_{24}(h+12)}{\gamma_{24}(h)}
 =(-1)^h.
\]

It preserves the \(P^{12}\)-sign on even coefficient blocks and exchanges
that sign on odd blocks. This initially appeared to be a new
obstruction; cycle 127 shows that the AFK normalization repairs it
exactly.

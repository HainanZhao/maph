# SIC--Stark research cycle 81: adversarial audit and theorem boundary

## What is now unconditional

For the canonical dimension-eight conductor-three tuple:

- all lower-conductor values are unconditional;
- both primitive quartic orientations are unconditional by CM
  reinduction and the imaginary-quadratic Stark theorem;
- the complete analytic overlap table equals the exact algebraic packet;
- both predicted shifts pass exact trace/idempotency and 784-minor
  certificates.

Thus both \(0\) and \(1\) are shifts for the canonical tuple.

## Checks against the old dead ends

- The argument does not infer equality from decimal recognition alone.
  Unit existence and integrality come first from Stark's theorem.
- It does not promote Roblot's modulus equality to an oriented equality.
- It does not assume projective equivalence implies linear equivalence;
  equality is checked on all sixteen group elements.
- It does not ignore Euler factors; full conductors and exact Artin
  characters are matched.
- It does not assume two unrelated unit resolvents with equal decimals
  are equal; their norm identities are proved exactly in the common
  normal closure.

## Remaining scope

This closes the canonical conductor-three dimension-eight mechanism.  A
claim of the *complete* dimension-eight formal TCC for every admissible
tuple still requires a separate audit of the maximal-order
discriminant-five tuple and transport within each form discriminant.
That scope distinction should remain explicit when the paper is revised.

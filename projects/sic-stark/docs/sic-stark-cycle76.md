# SIC--Stark research cycle 76: the applicable proved theorem

Stark's rank-one conjecture is a theorem for abelian extensions of an
imaginary quadratic base.  Applied to \(E_b/M_6\), it gives an actual
unit \(\varepsilon_b\in E_b^\times\) whose conjugate logarithms produce
the oriented derivative:

\[
\zeta'_S(0,g)=-\frac1e\log|g(\varepsilon_b)|,
\qquad e=|\mu(E_b)|=2.
\]

Taking the quartic character Fourier transform gives the complex
orientation, not merely its absolute value.  The fields \(E_b\) have
two roots of unity, and their conductors contain multiple finite prime
divisors, so the resulting Stark element is a global unit in the form
needed here.

Primary references used in this audit are Stark's imaginary-quadratic
proof via the second Kronecker limit formula and the explicit
Siegel--Ramachandra/elliptic-unit formulation:

- <https://ashwiniyengar.github.io/files/Stark.pdf>
- <https://d-nb.info/1193049156/34>

This theorem is the new discreteness input.  Without it, additional
decimal agreement would remain circular.

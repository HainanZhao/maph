## What has now been ruled out

The following do not independently prove the missing equality:

- quadratic class-number quotients or subgroup norms;
- double-sine reflection, shift, multiplication, or conductor-lowering
  identities;
- reversal-invariant polynomial and regulator data;
- the scalar absolute-value cyclic-dilogarithm limit;
- numerical root isolation, even to arbitrary precision; or
- local divisibility by \(A\tau-\tau\), which is equivalent to TCC.

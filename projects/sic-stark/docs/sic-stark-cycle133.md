# SIC--Stark research cycle 133: beta-transform specialization

Date: 2026-07-28

The Sarkissian--Spiridonov two-gamma identity was specialized at

\[
 (p,k,r,s)=(-115,24,5,24),\qquad g=Q,\qquad l=0.
\]

It gives the complete continuous--discrete Fourier transform of

\[
 K_Q(y,m)=\Gamma_M(y,m)\Gamma_M(Q-y,-m).
\]

Multiplication by \(5\) permutes all residues modulo \(24\), so no
discrete Fourier sector is omitted.  The factor
\(\Gamma_M(Q,0)\) is finite and nonzero by the exact divisor lattice.
The endpoint \(g=Q\) is reached by meromorphic continuation, not by an
unsupported direct-convergence assertion.

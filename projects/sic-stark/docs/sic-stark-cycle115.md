# SIC--Stark research cycle 115: a primitive common quantum torus

Date: 2026-07-28

## Outcome

Two of every three geodesic steps have odd denominator \(n\) coprime to
\(6\).  On this infinite subsequence, the order-\(n\) cyclic commutator
and the order-six Weyl commutator combine in the order-\(6n\) root
system with exponent

\[
 6m+n.
\]

Exact gcd calculation gives

\[
 \gcd(6m+n,6n)=1.
\]

Thus the combined commutator is primitive.  Equivalently, the
order-\(n\) cyclic torus and the level-six Weyl torus admit a canonical
CRT realization inside one irreducible order-\(6n\) torus.

This removes the earlier uncertainty about whether the two phases can be
put in a common quantum torus.  They can.

## Limitation

The \(36\) characteristic nodes form a sparse set, not a subgroup of
\(\mathbf Z/(6n)\).  Indeed \(36\nmid6n\) on the coprime subsequence,
and the certificate supplies an explicit failure of closure at every
tested step.  A direct subgroup quotient or unqualified partial trace
therefore cannot be the desired localization mechanism.

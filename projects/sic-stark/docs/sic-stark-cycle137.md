# SIC--Stark research cycle 137: TCC frequency match

Date: 2026-07-28

The two formal TCC shifts map output frequencies \((u,v)\) to beta
labels as follows:

\[
 \lambda=1:\quad (N,\ell)=(2-u,-u-v)\pmod6,
\]

\[
 \lambda=0:\quad (N,\ell)=(2-u-v,-v)\pmod6.
\]

Both maps are bijections of the \(36\) output sectors.  Hence the
helical beta transform covers exactly the frequency data needed by
both formal shifts.

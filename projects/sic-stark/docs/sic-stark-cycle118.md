# SIC--Stark research cycle 118: the fixed index-504 correspondence

Date: 2026-07-28

## Outcome

Although the cyclic orders \(n\) and \(n'\) vary, their defining lattice
correspondence has constant determinant.  If

\[
 A\binom mn=\binom{m'}{n'},
\qquad
 A=\begin{pmatrix}115&-24\\24&-5\end{pmatrix},
\]

then

\[
\begin{aligned}
 mn'-m'n
 &=24(m^2-5mn+n^2)\\
 &=-24\cdot21\\
 &=-504.
\end{aligned}
\]

The norm equation is exact on every geodesic step.  The four entries
have gcd one, so the lattice quotient has Smith invariants

\[
 (1,504).
\]

Thus the inter-level problem is governed by one fixed finite
correspondence rather than unrelated growing orders.

## What the cyclic quotient sees

Reduction modulo six retains the determinant pairing

\[
 (a,b)\longmapsto bm-an\pmod6,
\]

which is exactly the central-sector label from cycle 113.  The cyclic
quotient alone therefore sees six sectors and forgets the transverse
six-valued coordinate.  The missing coordinate must come from the
Heisenberg representation of the correspondence, not from the cyclic
quotient by itself.

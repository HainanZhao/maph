# SIC--Stark research cycle 52: candidate ray-unit class polynomials

## Result

The positive ray units have unexpectedly small reciprocal class
polynomials.

For the two quadratic strata:

\[
\begin{aligned}
P_+(x)&=x^4-2x^3-5x^2-2x+1,\\
P_-(x)&=x^4-8x^3-14x^2-8x+1.
\end{aligned}
\]

Their reciprocal traces are

\[
u_++u_+^{-1}=1+2\sqrt2,\qquad
u_-+u_-^{-1}=4+4\sqrt2.
\]

For scalar modulus \(7\), numerical recognition gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
P_7(x)={}&x^6-(10+6\sqrt2)x^5+(58+40\sqrt2)x^4\\
&-(129+90\sqrt2)x^3+(58+40\sqrt2)x^2\\
&-(10+6\sqrt2)x+1.
\end{aligned}
\]

For scalar modulus \(14\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{14}(x)={}&x^{12}-(32+22\sqrt2)x^{11}
 +(486+344\sqrt2)x^{10}\\
&-(3314+2344\sqrt2)x^9
 +(11956+8454\sqrt2)x^8\\
&-(25046+17710\sqrt2)x^7
 +(31899+22556\sqrt2)x^6\\
&-(25046+17710\sqrt2)x^5
 +(11956+8454\sqrt2)x^4\\
&-(3314+2344\sqrt2)x^3
 +(486+344\sqrt2)x^2\\
&-(32+22\sqrt2)x+1.
\end{aligned}
\]

Both relative polynomials have irreducible absolute equations.  PARI
verifies that their absolute fields are isomorphic to the exact modulus
\(7\) and modulus \(14\) ray fields, respectively.

## Proof status

The coefficient recognition residuals are below \(3\cdot10^{-168}\), and
the resulting exact fields match.  This is exceptionally strong evidence
but is not yet a proof that each analytic special value is the claimed
isolated root.  A rigorous interval/root-isolation certificate is the next
required step.

`scripts/dimension_seven_unit_polynomials.gp`; exact field checks are in
`scripts/dimension_seven_ray_fields.gp`.

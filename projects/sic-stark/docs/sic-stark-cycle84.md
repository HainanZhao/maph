# SIC--Stark research cycle 84: characteristic-to-ray dictionary

For a characteristic \((p,q)/8\), choose the unique lift \(p^\star\)
with
\[
q\beta'-p^\star>0,\qquad
\beta=\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2},\quad \beta'=\beta^{-1}.
\]
The corresponding principal element is
\[
\gamma=q\beta-p^\star.
\]
Reducing the common ideal divisor of \((8)\) and \((\gamma)\) produces
finite moduli \(8,4,2\).  The exact audit records, for all 63
nonzero characteristics, the positive lift, reduced modulus, ray log,
sign-class log, and differenced partial-zeta derivative.

An important correction occurs at denominator four.  There the
primitive stabilizer is \(C^3\), whereas \(A_t=C^6=(C^3)^2\).
The reduced ray logarithm must therefore be multiplied by two.
At denominator two the cocycle is \(\pm1\).

This explains the lower-conductor factor that was missing in the first
exploratory reconstruction.

# SIC--Stark research cycle 37: the exact \(p\)-adic analogue is conjectural

Date: 2026-07-27

## Outcome

There is a \(p\)-adic theory aimed at precisely this signature.
Ferrara constructs a \(p\)-adic \(L\)-function for finite-order
mixed-signature characters of real quadratic fields and formulates a
rank-one \(p\)-adic Stark conjecture.

Our primitive character has exactly that form.  Its induction to
\mathbb Q\) is an odd dihedral Artin representation, so the associated
weight-one/modular-symbol machinery is the correct \(p\)-adic setting.

However, Ferrara proves the conjecture in the imaginary-quadratic split
case, not in the mixed-signature real-quadratic case.  In that latter case
the \(p\)-adic function has no classical critical values to interpolate,
and even its canonical period normalization is part of the difficulty.

Recent work on \(p\)-adic iterated integrals and Gross--Stark units also
treats real-quadratic theta series, but the required formulas remain
conjectural or experimental in the relevant mixed-signature cases.

## Assessment

A \(p\)-adic computation could independently identify the same algebraic
unit \(x^2\) and would be worthwhile as evidence.  It would not prove the
archimedean identity without an additional comparison theorem between
the \(p\)-adic and complex Stark regulators.  The newest common-refinement
proposals formulate such a comparison in period rings but do not yet give
an unconditional theorem for this example.

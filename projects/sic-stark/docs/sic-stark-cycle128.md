# SIC--Stark research cycle 128: the even wrap is a Fourier sector

Date: 2026-07-28

The residual chirp

\[
 g_b(a)=\zeta_{12}^{a^2+\kappa_ba}
\]

is periodic in \(a\bmod6\) for even \(b\) and antiperiodic for odd
\(b\). Consequently the correct Fourier frequency is

\[
 k\quad(b\ \text{even}),\qquad
 k+\frac12\quad(b\ \text{odd}).
\]

With this parity correction, exact cyclotomic reduction proves

\[
 \left|
 \sum_{a=0}^{5}
 \zeta_{12}^{a^2+\kappa_ba-(2k+(b\bmod2))a}
 \right|^2=6
\]

for every \(b,k\bmod6\).

The even-dimensional wrap sign is therefore the metaplectic
half-frequency correction. It is exactly what makes all six Fourier
kernels flat, rather than an auxiliary convention.

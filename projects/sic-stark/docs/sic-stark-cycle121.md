## Missing theorem

What remains is an analytic operator statement:

> Construct the regularized Shintani--Faddeev transfer operator on the
> level-24 or level-504 Heisenberg representation, prove that it
> preserves the canonical trivial-central-character block, and identify
> its restriction with the AFK ghost matrix.

If that restriction is an idempotent by an inversion/pentagon relation,
dimension-six TCC follows without separately proving the mixed-signature
Stark algebraicity identity.

## Why published theorems do not yet supply it

- Ishibashi's 2026 pentagon is finite and fixed-level.
- Yalkinoglu's theorem is an inter-level scalar absolute-value limit.
- The general modular quantum dilogarithm works on
  \(\mathbf R\times\mathbf Z/24\) and retains a continuous integral.
- The earlier localization audit proves the AFK samples lie in the
  pole-free strip, so the standard residue argument does not restrict
  that integral to the six-dimensional block.

The missing step is therefore a real-multiplication block-restriction
theorem, not another phase convention or scalar asymptotic.

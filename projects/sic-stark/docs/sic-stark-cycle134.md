# SIC--Stark research cycle 134: the legitimate helical quotient

Date: 2026-07-28

The two individual functional shifts generate a dense subgroup of
\(\mathbb R\times\mathbb Z/24\), so they cannot define a topological
Zak lattice.  The AFK grid uses only their difference

\[
 T=(\omega_1-\omega_2,6).
\]

The cyclic subgroup \(\langle T\rangle\) is discrete and cocompact.
Thus

\[
 X=(\mathbb R\times\mathbb Z/24)/\langle T\rangle
\]

is the correct compact helical quotient.

# SIC--Stark research cycle 104: current linear-regulator literature

Date: 2026-07-28

## Search target

The preceding cycles leave one possible modular solution:

> a theorem linear in the original level-\(756\) representation whose
> regulator retains the \(\chi_1\) anti-invariant component.

This cycle checked the closest current results through July 2026.

## Results checked

1. The unified Harris--Venkatesh/Stark framework proves compatibility
   for **imaginary dihedral** weight-one forms and evaluates adjoint
   \(L\)-values through elliptic units
   ([arXiv:2305.08956](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.08956)).
   Our form is uniquely real-dihedral, and cycle 103 proves that its
   adjoint has already discarded the target line.

2. The modular algorithms for Gross--Stark units compute
   \(p\)-adic logarithms using overconvergent modular forms
   ([arXiv:2301.08977](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08977)).
   They do not prove the complex identity
   \(L'_S(0,\chi_1)=R_{\mathrm{alg}}\).

3. Kashio's 2025 common-refinement paper constructs period-ring-valued
   functions under a generalized Yoshida period conjecture and proves
   the full reciprocity statement over \(\mathbf Q\), with partial
   results over other real fields
   ([arXiv:1706.03198](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03198);
   [JLMS 111 (2025), e70147](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.70147)).
   The unconditional real-field shortcut highlighted there requires the
   relevant narrow ray field to be abelian over \(\mathbf Q\).

The dimension-six ray field fails the last hypothesis: its normal closure
has group \([24,8]\), and the faithful projective quotient is
\([12,4]\simeq D_{12}\).  It is not abelian over \(\mathbf Q\).
The broader period statement is conjectural.

## Conclusion

\[
\boxed{\text{no current linear regulator theorem covers the
level-\(756\) mixed-signature form.}}
\]

This is stronger than a keyword-level literature gap: each nearby theorem
misses an exact certified property—real rather than imaginary induction,
complex rather than \(p\)-adic value, nonabelian absolute closure, or the
anti-invariant rather than adjoint component.

# SIC--Stark research cycle 35: ETNC does not supply the phase

Date: 2026-07-27

## Question

Does a modern unconditional form of the equivariant Tamagawa number
conjecture or strong Stark conjecture imply the oriented complex equality
left after cycle 34?

## Finding

No located theorem covers this extension.

The strongest recent unconditional results apply to minus parts of
abelian **CM** extensions of totally real fields, or to totally odd
characters under specified ramification hypotheses.  The dimension-six
ray field is not CM:

\[
 \operatorname{sig}(L)=(6,3).
\]

Its primitive character has mixed signature—odd at one real embedding
and even at the other.  Consequently it is outside the CM/minus framework
of the unconditional strong Brumer--Stark and ETNC results.

The generalized Iwasawa-main-conjecture results for monomial Artin motives
formulate an appropriate route, but their implication of the relevant
Tamagawa statement depends on new Iwasawa-theoretic and extra-zero
conjectures.  Monomiality alone is not an unconditional Stark theorem.

## Conclusion

ETNC correctly predicts the missing phase-sensitive equality, but
currently relocates rather than proves it.  Cycle 34's elementary P1
certificate is stronger for this particular example than any directly
applicable published ETNC result, although it still yields only absolute
value.

# SIC--Stark research cycle 135: Pontryagin dual and aliases

Date: 2026-07-28

A character

\[
 \chi_{\xi,n}(y,m)=e^{2\pi i\xi y}\omega_{24}^{nm}
\]

descends to the helical quotient exactly when

\[
 \xi(\omega_1-\omega_2)+\frac n4=\ell\in\mathbb Z.
\]

Its restriction to the AFK subgroup has frequency

\[
 (p_a,p_b)=(-n,\ell)\pmod 6.
\]

Every finite frequency therefore collects four discrete lifts and
infinitely many continuous lifts.  A single beta-transform value can
never equal a finite TCC coefficient; an alias theorem is essential.

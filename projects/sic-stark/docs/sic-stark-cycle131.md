# SIC--Stark research cycle 131: exact beta-kernel identification

Date: 2026-07-28

The canonical general modular parameters are

\[
 (p,k,r,s)=(-115,24,5,24),
 \qquad pr+ks=1.
\]

Let \(Z(h)\) be the normalization multiplier of the rarefied hyperbolic
gamma. Exact reduction gives

\[
 \frac{Z(h+4)}{Z(h)}=\tau_6^h.
\]

Therefore the primitive AFK quotient is

\[
\boxed{
 \frac{\gamma_M(\mu,h)}{\gamma_M(\mu,h+4)}
 =
 \tau_6^h
 \Gamma_M(\mu,h)\Gamma_M(Q-\mu,-h).
}
\]

The product on the right is exactly the kernel

\[
 \Gamma_M(y,m)\Gamma_M(-y+g,\ell-m)
\]

in the published Sarkissian--Spiridonov degenerate beta convolution,
with \(y=\mu,m=h,g=Q,\ell=0\). The two normalized factors have matching
quasiperiod signs, so their product is genuinely periodic modulo \(24\).

The remaining issue is no longer identification of the amplitude. It is
the finite Zak descent of that published continuous-discrete
convolution.

# C78 paper-phase literature and overlap audit

Audit date: 2026-08-05.

## Exact overlap

- **PROVED:** Zhiwei Song and Lin Chen, *A counterexample to the strong spin
  alignment conjecture*, arXiv:2603.25410v1, 26 March 2026, formulate the
  compatible-marginal conjecture as Conjecture 2 and prove the identical
  three-qubit, pair-support \(Q=I/2\) endpoint as Proposition 3. Their proof
  contains the ordered-prefix decomposition, the polygon reduction, the
  spin-flip expansion, lower-tail Ky Fan bound, and target assembly. C79 is
  an independently certified reconstruction replacing their use of a
  two-summand citation with a direct principal-angle calculation; it is not
  a mechanism-level novelty claim.
- **PROVED:** Their conclusion identifies arbitrary positive semidefinite
  \(Q\) in more general three-qubit settings as a natural next step. The
  manuscript proves the qubit, same-pair-support portion only.
- **PROVED:** Higuchi--Sudbery--Szulc, *One-qubit reduced states of a pure
  many-qubit state: polygon inequalities*, Physical Review Letters 90,
  107902 (2003), Eq. (2), p. 1, gives exactly the smaller-eigenvalue polygon
  inequalities used after the pure-state Ky Fan reduction.
- **PROVED:** Alhejji--Knill, *Towards a resolution of the spin alignment
  problem*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 405, 119 (2024),
  Proposition IV.5, proves a stronger classical state-tuple result than the
  program's diagonal consistency check.

## Scoped novelty screen

**OBSERVED:** An exact-statement, arXiv-ID/title, current-primary/citation
screen through this audit date found no separate theorem for arbitrary qubit
\(Q\) with compatible three-qubit pair marginals and arbitrary pair weights.
Song--Chen remains v1-only. This bounded screen does not establish priority,
absence, or publishability. The manuscript may say only that it is unaware of
such a separate result as of this date.

## Citation boundary

The paper must credit Song--Chen for the parent conjecture, the endpoint, and
the stated arbitrary-\(Q\) direction. It must cite HSS by its exact
equation/page and distinguish the ordinary majorization order from Loewner
order. No claim of a new endpoint proof, broad compatible-marginal theorem,
or priority is authorized.

# C82 idea selection: LEM inverse realization

**Question.** Can a parametrized poset family of order at least 15 realize a
full LEM 4-cycle using a comparable edge while its incomparable-only digraph
has no 4-cycle?

**Question the question.** A direct census cannot answer the mechanism and
Gupta's exhaustive agreement through order 14 makes it especially poor value.
The inherited assumption that a correlation inequality must prove the result
is misleading: C81 supplied exact abstract countermodels to both dominance and
XYZ-only versions. The discriminating question is realization, not another
inequality variant.

**Candidates.** (1) inverse modular realization: replace the four marked
vertices by asymmetric chain/antichain modules and derive exact extension
generating functions; (2) marked order-ideal flow cone; (3) renewed census.
Choose (1): it can either produce a counterexample or an exact infeasibility
certificate for a named family. Reject (3) as non-mechanistic; defer (2),
which is higher-dimensional and only a relaxation.

**Falsifier and advance condition.** A valid hit has exact pair margins, a
full 4-cycle, and no incomparable-only 4-cycle. An exact sign-infeasibility
certificate for the frozen family ends that family without broadening it.

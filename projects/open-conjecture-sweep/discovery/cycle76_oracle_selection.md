## Selection — CONJECTURED planning decision

Select Song--Chen Conjecture 2 in the restricted next case: three qubits,
uniform weights on \(AB,AC,BC\), and
\(Q=\operatorname{diag}(q,1-q)\) with rational \(q>1/2\).

- **First gate:** decide whether a rational normalized pure state and rational
  \(q\) violate one Ky Fan inequality.  Any candidate requires exact
  characteristic-polynomial/root-isolation or an invariant-subspace
  certificate.
- **Falsifier:** explicit \(q\), state, Ky Fan index \(k\), and a rigorous
  positive margin \(K_k(H)-K_k(H_{\rm target})>0\).
- **Strongest flaw:** numerical optimization near spectral degeneracies can
  create false positives, while the restricted family may have a hidden
  symmetry.  A null random search has no positive consequence.
- **Expected information:** high: an exact violation refutes the full
  compatible-marginal conjecture; a defensible local rigidity result requires
  a separate analytic strengthening and audit.
- **50-cycle pivot:** stop by 50 attack cycles unless there is an exact
  violation, a proof of this restricted gate, or a lower-dimensional analytic
  reduction with a falsifiable closure route.  Otherwise run the required
  closure postmortem and return to the LEM 4-cycle question.

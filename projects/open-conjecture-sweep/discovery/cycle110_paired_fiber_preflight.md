# C110 paired-fiber preflight containment

**`PROVED` algebraic defect, no executable C110 work.**  The proposed
cross-fiber formula counts common neighbours in third fibers only.  For a
cross-fiber edge, the other vertex in either endpoint fiber can also be a
common neighbour: its within-fiber adjacency was explicitly left free, while
its cross-fiber adjacency is fixed.  These two omitted contributions can alter
the red and blue counts.  Therefore the asymmetric caps do not force
\(t_{xy}=-1\), and \(H^2+H=(q-1)I\) is not a necessary condition for the
declared state.

No C110 executable code, q=7 enumeration, or result exists.  The
preregistration is retained only as a failed pre-execution design; a future
paired-fiber method must first freeze within-fiber colours and rederive all
caps including their contributions.

# C89 selection: rank-one escape geometry for the Möbius ladder

## Creative comparison

`CONJECTURED` candidates were: a rank-one local escape analysis for
\(H=K_{5,5}\setminus C_{10}\); a Gold-obstructed prime-poset blocking-ideal
separator; a non-greedy tuple potential for intersecting Ryser at \(r=6\);
and a Frankl blocker-polytope face theorem.  The poset option has unresolved
overlap with Gupta's new Gold-certificate machinery.  The Ryser and Frankl
options presently lack a sharp finite verifier after C87/C88 and C86.

## Question → question the questioning → brainstorm

The inherited question "is a Hessian PSD?" is insufficient: a nonconstant
rank-one base has positive deficit and need not be stationary under
density-preserving perturbations.  Favoring a rational Hessian merely because
it is computable would confuse negative curvature with a Sidorenko
counterexample.  The actual gate therefore separates exact first variation,
restricted quadratic curvature, and the full feasible-line deficit
polynomial.

Oracle selected the `CONJECTURED` **rank-one escape geometry** mechanism:
on a frozen rational \(3\times3\) step bigraphon, take an interior rank-one
base \(W_{0,ij}=r_i c_j\), restrict to density-tangent directions, then to
directions with zero first deficit variation.  Check the restricted Hessian
by exact rational congruence/LDL and, for any negative direction, check the
full degree-15 deficit over its exact box-feasible \(\epsilon\)-interval.

- **Falsifier:** a rational feasible direction with zero first variation and
  negative second variation falsifies the PSD mechanism; it threatens
  Sidorenko only if an exact feasible \(\epsilon\) makes the full deficit
  negative.
- **Expected information:** high about whether a local route beyond the
  global SOS barrier survives; moderate about the full conjecture.
- **Strongest flaw:** positive rank-one slack can make a curvature failure
  noncrossing and hence strategically sterile.
- **Stop/pivot:** one symbolic/algebra cap and a small exact packet only.  No
  larger 3-step grid, pair of nearby bases, or post-result threshold repair.
  Continue only with a certified negative full deficit or a source-cleared
  parameter-uniform Gram theorem having a credible extension mechanism.

# C57 idea selection: conditional variance under one-class smoothing

1. **Coefficient pairing (chosen).** Write (a=s+b), where (s=S_Ca) and
   (b) is supported on (C) with class sum zero. Expand the degree-15
   Cayley numerator in (b), then pair assignments under simultaneous
   conjugation. Seek a representation in differences (b(g)-b(h)) times
   contractions of (s). A sign-indefinite coefficient is a compact
   obstruction to the proposed universal smoothing proof.

2. **More finite groups.** Rejected: C56 already supplies the cheap
   falsifier packet and no finite pass proves the group-uniform identity.

3. **Assume Schur convexity.** Rejected: simultaneous conjugation does not
   permit independent permutations of values within a class.

The question may fail because high-degree mixed terms need not pair with a
positive coefficient. The sharp falsifier is an explicit symbolic coefficient
whose sign can be negative under nonnegative (s), or a rational group
function realizing that sign.

# C58 idea selection: Gram squares for class smoothing

1. **Frozen Gram family (chosen).** Factor the (S_3) 3-cycle deficit by
   (t^2), use the homogeneous degree-(13) remainder, and seek a PSD Gram
   representation in low-degree class/deviation monomials, modulo the simplex
   multiplier (c_C^2-t^2). Gram squares permit negative raw coefficients,
   avoiding C57's exact cone obstruction.

2. **Enrich the Handelman cone.** Rejected: C57 closed its fixed mechanism;
   adding generators would be an exception ladder rather than a new proof
   principle.

3. **More group searches.** Rejected: they cannot prove smoothing and C56
   already supplied a finite falsifier packet.

The decisive flaw is PSD failure of the frozen Gram family. A numerical PSD
fit is discovery only; promotion requires an exact rational Gram matrix and
exact coefficient identity. A rational negative smoothing point would instead
falsify the route itself.

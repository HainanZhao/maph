# Cycle 43 idea selection: actual moment classes in ambient H2

## Brainstorm

1. Couple the globally canonical Cycle 41 triple moments to every Cycle 42
   four-partite complex, then solve the coherent face-choice system if the
   canonical assignment encounters a nonboundary class.
2. Infer a chain-homotopy theorem immediately from the first filled moment
   cycle.
3. Expand the ambient topology census to every four-type tuple.
4. Stop Problem 1 and bank the remaining allocation.

## Decision questions

- For 1: does any actual oriented moment two-cycle have nonzero rational
  homology, and if so is that obstruction intrinsic or removable by coherent
  choices among valid Cycle 41 triple fills?
- For 2: what natural transformation is presently defined on all faces, and
  what would falsify its claimed homotopy identity?
- For 3: would more ambient homology matter before it is paired with actual
  moments?
- For 4: has the new mechanism run out of a sharp exact test, or is the frozen
  coupling census precisely such a test?

## Questioning the questioning

Cycle 42 shows why “is H2 nonzero?” was only the prototype question. Ambient
classes are abundant, yet the first actual moment cycle is a one-tetrahedron
boundary. The informative object is the homology class of the signed moment
cycle, not the homology group in isolation.

A single canonical failure would also be too weak if reported as a degree-four
obstruction: Cycle 41's valid triple fills form affine spaces, and another
globally coherent choice may move the face cycle into the tetrahedral image.
The correct fallback is therefore a shared-variable system over triple-fill
freedoms and four-way fills, not another local selector.

An abstract chain homotopy is deferred until the complete selected census
shows whether it is even true. Expanding to all quadruples is rejected because
it multiplies ambient data without answering the coupling question. Stopping
is premature because both the canonical test and its coherent generalization
have exact falsifiers and bounded state spaces.

## Choice and falsifier

Choose the full selected moment--H2 coupling census. Define one deterministic
lowest-pivot tensor for each unordered face from the Cycle 41 pair moments,
average over its repeated-type stabilizer, and transport that single tensor to
every occurrence. Test all 3,954 oriented four-face cycles against exact
tetrahedral boundaries. If any canonical cycle is nonboundary, retain its
dual cochain and escalate to the joint coherent face-choice system across all
selected interfaces.

Falsifier: inconsistent use of a shared face tensor, failed repeated-type
symmetry, a wrong pair marginal, nonzero oriented boundary, a claimed fill
that does not reproduce the cycle, a claimed obstruction whose cochain does
not annihilate every tetrahedral boundary, or an independent replay mismatch.

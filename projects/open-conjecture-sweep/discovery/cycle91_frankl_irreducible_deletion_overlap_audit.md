# C91 Frankl irreducible-deletion overlap audit

`PROVED` from Bouchard, *On the lattice formulation of the union-closed sets
conjecture*, Lemma 1.2: deleting a join- or meet-irreducible preserves the
lattice property.  Theorem 1.4 iterates such deletion over every set of
join-irreducibles; Theorems 2.1--2.6 derive exactly the
minimum-counterexample up-set constraints that the proposed C91 induction
would use.

Therefore the proposed “lattice irreducible-deletion induction” is not a new
mechanism or a discriminating first gate.  A small deletion census could only
reproduce these necessary conditions or an already-known counterexample class.

## Decision

Reject this candidate before preregistration and execution.  Any future Frankl
selection must specify an invariant beyond irreducible deletion and Bouchard's
up-set-count transport.

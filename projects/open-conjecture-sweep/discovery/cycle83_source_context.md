# C83 source context: LEM comparable-edge four-cycles

`OBSERVED` from Anish Gupta, *Balance Constants, Majority Cycles, and the Gold
Partition Conjecture through Fourteen Elements*, arXiv:2607.23926v2 (30 July
2026), read 5 August 2026:

- Section 4.1 defines \(D(P)\) as the incomparable-pair majority relation
  plus the strict order relation (lines 181--185 of the arXiv HTML).
- Proposition 7 proves only that a directed triangle cannot use a comparable
  edge (lines 186--191).  The paper explicitly says the bypass argument for a
  longer cycle need not yield a contradiction (line 192).
- The paper computes full and restricted cycle spectra as separate counters;
  its agreement through order 14 is a finite exact census, not a universal
  spectrum theorem (lines 192--203 and Question 14 in Section 8).
- Its ideal-lattice recurrence computes exact pair counts (lines 119--135),
  but is an established all-pairs algorithm rather than a ready-made
  comparable-edge identity.

This confirms that C83's triangle-tip reduction overlaps neither a stated
general theorem nor a source-approved extension of Proposition 7.  It does
not establish novelty: the paper is a current preprint and this is only one
primary source.

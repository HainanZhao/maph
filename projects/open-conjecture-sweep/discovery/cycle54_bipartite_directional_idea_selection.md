# C54 idea selection: the full bipartite tangent space

## Candidates

1. **Two-sided degree/kernel extension (chosen).** Replace C53's symmetric
   kernel by a bounded measurable bipartite kernel
   \(U:X\times Y\to[-1,1]\).  The quadratic edge-pair contribution splits
   into left- and right-degree squares.  On the simultaneous degree-zero
   kernel, leaf cancellation leaves the same five four-cycles, but each has
   value \(\operatorname{tr}((T_UT_U^*)^2)=\sum s_i^4>0\).  The exact
   edge-subset census is inherited as a frozen combinatorial input; C54 tests
   the new analytic, nonsymmetric bridge.

2. **Quantitative uniform-remainder bound.** A uniform radius would be more
   useful but is not implied by C53 and risks conflating tiny quadratic and
   quartic leading forms with a global lower bound.  It is deferred until the
   broader directional theorem is sound.

3. **Global finite graphon search.** It remains the eventual global engine,
   but should not precede the full tangent-space reduction.  It is deferred.

## Question the question

Does an asymmetric bipartite kernel prove anything about an ordinary symmetric
graphon?  It proves a stronger bipartite-kernel local statement, but does not
turn every global symmetric competitor into a small perturbation.  The
question would be misleading if the two-sided leaf cancellation required
symmetry; it does not, provided both marginals vanish.  The discriminating
falsifier is an edge-subset term whose integral cannot be eliminated by one
of the two degree functions, or a four-cycle term with nonpositive singular-
value expression.

## Choice

Choose the two-sided extension.  It is a distinct theorem question—not a
larger C52 census—and identifies the correct full bipartite local tangent
space before any nonlocal extremizer search.

# Cycle 25 idea selection scratch

Candidate A — **quadratic-character CRT dual (chosen).** Split the
nonzero alpha class into quadratic residues and nonresidues.  Tensor those
three alpha types with the four beta gcd classes, yielding twelve exact
integer-valued classes.  This is the smallest real-character refinement of
Cycle 24 and directly tests its 58/60 alpha-collapse.

Candidate B — more partitions under the eight-class dual. Rejected: it
changes Cycle 24's frozen one-partition selection family without addressing
its class degeneracy, so it is lower-information than A.

Candidate C — width-five heterogeneous LP. Rejected until the compact
quadratic refinement has been tested; its option growth is materially higher.

Questioning the choice: quadratic character may still be invisible to direct
coverage and yield the same single-class dual. That negative result would
not rule out individual characters, higher degree, width five, or a semantic
primal approach. It is chosen because the convention, class census, and
integer capacity replay are small and falsifiable.

Chosen question: does the twelve-class quadratic CRT space produce a strict
direct integer deficit on one of the 60 named leaves?  Main rejected
alternative: a width-five LP.  Falsifier: a convention/direct-mask mismatch,
or full frozen-family failure to obtain U<W.

# Oracle C115 selection: paired Paley-tournament lift

**`CONJECTURED` planning decision; F001 only.** Oracle selected a
transpose-compatible noncirculant 1/3-edge lift comparing the Paley tournament
\(T\) with \(U=T^\pi\), rather than another scalar cross kernel. The state
has no within-fiber red edge; for \(x\ne y\), the \(x,y\) fiber block has
three red edges when \(T_{xy}=U_{xy}\), with the one excluded endpoint pair
as specified by the two tournament signs, and one red edge otherwise.

The intended degree-seven invariant is two common out-neighbours and two
common in-neighbours for every vertex in \(T,U\). It differs from C107/C109/
C111/C112 kernels, C113 matching fibers, and C114 circulant blocks. Its
falsifier is a balance survivor with a direct cap survivor or a
formula/reconstruction disagreement; a no-hit would only concern this lift.

# C61 idea selection: invariant flat strata

At central bases where C60's kernel Hessian vanishes, do not assume quartic
positivity. The transposition kernel is the (S_3) standard representation,
whose invariant ring permits a cubic. Choose the exact flat-stratum test:
derive curvature zero loci, restrict to the full kernel, and test the cubic
first. A nonzero cubic reverses sign under direction reversal and gives a
rational local Zhao-route countermodel.

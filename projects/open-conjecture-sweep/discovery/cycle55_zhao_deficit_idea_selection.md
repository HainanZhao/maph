# C55 idea selection: symbolic (S_3) conjugacy-deficit rays

## Candidates

1. **Frozen symbolic (S_3) ray packet (chosen).**  Write a nonnegative
   function as \(a=c+tb\), where \(c\) is class-constant and \(b\) has zero
   sum on every conjugacy class.  Then \(a^{\rm cl}=c\), so the Zhao
   comparison is exactly the sign of one polynomial
   \(t_{\rm Cay}(H;c+tb)-t_{\rm Cay}(H;c)\).  Use the smallest nonabelian
   group, a compact primitive integer packet in the conjugacy-averaging
   kernel, and exact dyadic rays.  A negative row gives a compact rational
   falsifier of this global-bridge route.

2. **Attempt a universal character formula immediately.**  It could prove
   the bridge, but the degree-15 deficit has many coupled representation
   blocks. Without a smallest-block sign diagnostic, an elegant formula risks
   hiding a false positivity premise. Deferred pending the packet.

3. **Expand the prior finite-group census.**  It gives little information
   after C51: more groups or arbitrary indicators is a census treadmill.
   Rejected. C55 instead fixes one representation-theoretic kernel and emits
   the complete ray polynomials.

## Question the question

Why use only (S_3)? A pass cannot establish Zhao's all-group hypothesis.
Why could the packet be misleading? A negative direction outside its small
integer rays may exist, while an interior dyadic evaluation can miss a
polynomial sign change. Therefore all coefficients are retained, and the
only promoted positive statement is the exact frozen packet. The decisive
falsifier is a nonnegative rational row with a negative exact deficit, not a
numerical minimum.

## Choice

Use class values \(c_e,c_T,c_C\in\{1,2\}\), primitive sign-canonical
class-zero directions \(b\in\{-1,0,1\}^{S_3}\), and rays
\(t=r/8\), \(-8\le r\le8\).  This provides a small exact block diagnostic
and makes any failure independently replayable.

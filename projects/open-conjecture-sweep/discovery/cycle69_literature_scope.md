# C69 literature scope

`PROVED` source facts checked in the cited primary texts:

- Haxell--Scott, *On Ryser's Conjecture*, p. 2, states that the intersecting
  case is open for every \(r\ge6\), and quotes Tuza's \(r\le5\) theorem.
- Abu-Khazneh--Pokrovskiy, Section 2.2, explicitly gives a 13-edge
  intersecting 6-partite hypergraph with \(\tau=5\) and proves \(f(6)=13\).
- Aharoni--Barat--Wanless (2016) reports a fractional version of the stronger
  edge-or-one-class matching-reduction statement, while the corresponding
  integral stronger form fails for \(r>3\).

Consequences for C69: the target is presently eligible; an argument that only
repackages fractional matching/cover duality is unlikely to close the integral
\(r=6\) case.  The 13-edge equality construction is a mandatory exact
control, not a candidate counterexample.

Sources: Haxell--Scott (2012), pp. 2--3; Abu-Khazneh--Pokrovskiy (2017),
Section 2.2 and Appendix A; Aharoni--Barat--Wanless (2016), abstract.

## Questioning the questioning

Automatically asking “what about degree three?” would create an endless
ladder of method no-gos. Degree two is special as one bounded discriminator:
it is the first test that can delete two ordinary zero contractions at once
and therefore distinguishes a genuine robust annihilator from the Cycle 36
condition.

For a rank-one product functional and distinct-coordinate multipliers of size
at most two, all generators based on \(F_t\) vanish exactly when either at
least three local contractions are zero or one zero coordinate is strong
pointwise. If there are three zeros, removing two leaves one. A strong zero
survives whether its coordinate is multiplied or not. Conversely, with at
most two zeros and no strong one, multiply at every zero coordinate and choose
options with nonzero local point factors to obtain a nonzero generator. This
theorem compresses over sixteen million raw pair labels to 1,394 predicate
conditions.

## Choice, rejected alternative, falsifier

Choose Idea 1 as a single capped prototype. Reject arbitrary degree-two
linear algebra as premature, and defer ownership/transfer until the exact
escape profile is known. Do not infer authorization for a further automatic
degree climb.

A positive functional is falsified by any nonzero raw generator or nonunit
mass. A negative exhaustive claim is falsified by an omitted ordinary/strong
addition. Failure rejects only this rank-one product ansatz, not arbitrary
degree-two duals or certificates.

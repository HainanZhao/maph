# C66 idea selection: cross-conjecture leverage of the S3 reduction

## Serious candidates

1. **Bounded novelty-and-leverage audit for the S3 Zhao comparison (chosen).**
   Read the primary theorem and its proof interface, then search current
   primary literature for the exact `S3`, conjugacy-averaging, orbit-quotient,
   and finite-group special cases.  Decide whether C63--C64 state a new
   theorem that materially advances a named open condition and identify the
   smallest missing closure lemma.

   - Preserves: Zhao's exact comparison direction and hypotheses, the frozen
     Möbius graph, and C64's exact claim boundary.
   - Falsifier: a prior theorem already subsumes the `S3` case or shows that
     the reduction has no implication beyond a routine computation.
   - Advance: a checked novelty gap plus a precise open theorem whose proof
     would use the degree-26 reduction, with one falsifiable next engine.

2. **Immediate exact CAD/Bernstein closure of the outer three-dimensional
   continuum.**  C64 makes this technically conceivable.  Rejected for now:
   without a novelty and implication audit, an expensive sign certificate may
   prove an already known or strategically inert special case.

3. **Proceed directly to intersecting Ryser at `r=6`.**  This remains the
   portfolio default.  Deferred for one bounded audit because the user's new
   pivot rule gives already-proved leverage priority over a cold start.

4. **Resume larger step-graphon or larger-group searches.**  Rejected.  They
   are variants of exhausted falsifier ladders and do not exploit C64's
   theorem.

## Question the questioning

Why should one finite group matter when Zhao's sufficient condition quantifies
over every finite group?  It matters only if the `S3` proof exposes a
transferable invariant or is itself a nontrivial open special case.  A bare
finite-group verification is not enough.

Why audit before doing algebra?  “Useful to an open conjecture” is a stricter
standard than thematic relevance.  We need the theorem number, exact logical
implication, prior-art boundary, and missing lemma before allocating another
research block.

What inherited framing may mislead us?  The project has treated the Möbius
graph as the only target.  C63--C64 may instead be more naturally framed as a
finite-group conjugacy-averaging theorem.  Conversely, calling that a pivot
could exaggerate its value if Zhao's proof needs uniform control that an
isolated `S3` case cannot inform.

What does this question hide?  It delays the fresh Ryser portfolio entry.  The
delay is justified only for one bounded literature-and-logic audit; failure of
the advance condition returns immediately to Problem 3.

## Chosen question

Do C63--C64 provide a novel, materially useful reduction for a precisely named
open Zhao conjugacy-averaging problem, and if so what smallest falsifiable
closure lemma should replace the exhausted search ladders?

Main rejected alternative: immediate CAD/Bernstein closure without checking
novelty or logical leverage.

Falsifier: checked primary literature subsumes the `S3` result, or exact
logical tracing shows that completing it supplies neither a nontrivial open
special case nor a transferable mechanism.

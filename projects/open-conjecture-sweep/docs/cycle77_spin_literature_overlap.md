# C77 literature overlap: classical spin alignment

## Finding — PROVED prior overlap

M. A. Alhejji and E. Knill, *Towards a resolution of the spin alignment
problem* (Communications in Mathematical Physics 405, 119 (2024)), Proposition
IV.5, proves the strong spin-alignment majorization statement whenever every
input state in the state tuple is classical (diagonal in the eigenbasis of
\(Q\)), for every probability measure \(\mu\).

Therefore C77's diagonal three-qubit, uniform-two-body compatible-marginal
lemma is an immediate specialization: take the tuple to be the compatible
marginals of a diagonal global state.  The C77 exact table is retained as a
replay and convention cross-check only.  It has no novelty, seal, or paper
claim.

## Consequence for the live gate

The remaining eligible C77 territory is coherent (off-diagonal) global states
or a compatibility-preserving reduction from general qubit \(Q\) to
\(Q=I/2\).  Do not promote the diagonal slice as a result.

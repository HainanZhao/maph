# C75 source audit: Holevo--Utkin norm conjecture

## Primary source

A. S. Holevo and A. V. Utkin, *A conjecture on a tight norm inequality in
the finite-dimensional \(l_p\)*, arXiv:2603.24017v2, revised 9 April 2026;
the arXiv record lists *Lobachevskii Journal of Mathematics* **47** (2026),
3300--3310. Source consulted 5 August 2026:
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24017>.

The paper formulates, for the zero-sum hyperplane and \(\|x\|_2=1\), the
extremum of \(F_\alpha(x)=\sum_i|x_i|^{2\alpha}\). It proves the case
\(d=3\), and its abstract describes a numerical check for \(d\leq200\),
not a proof for \(d=4\). Its numerical-verification discussion derives the
interior multiplier equation
\[
 2\alpha x|x|^{2(\alpha-1)}-2\lambda x-\mu=0,
\]
and observes that it has at most three real solutions. The paper does not
provide the complete \(d=4\) boundary-and-sign-strata proof proposed in C75.

## Eligibility boundary

**CORRECTION / OBSERVED.** This audit was incomplete. Haonan Zhang,
*Proof of the Holevo-Utkin conjecture on sharp \(\ell_p\) norms for
zero-sum vectors*, arXiv:2605.05243v1, dated 4 May 2026,
states in Theorem 2 that the conjecture holds for all \(d\ge4\):
<https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05243v1>. It directly includes the proposed
\(d=4\) target.

C75 is therefore an overlapping reconstruction, not a new proof, scoped
theorem, or paper candidate. No C75 result may be sealed or promoted. The
miss demonstrates that title/author search alone was an inadequate
eligibility screen; future selection must search the conjecture statement and
its arXiv citation trail before attack work begins.

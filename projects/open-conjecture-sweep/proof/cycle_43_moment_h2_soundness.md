# Cycle 43 soundness boundary

## Headline

`PROVED`: one globally shared, repeated-type-symmetric Cycle 41 face assignment
extends across every one of the 3,954 Cycle 42 selected four-type interfaces.
Each oriented moment two-cycle is an exact integral tetrahedral boundary,
although 3,893 of those interfaces have nonzero ambient rational H2.

“Canonical” here means deterministic lowest-pivot selection followed by
stabilizer averaging. It does not mean a natural chain map.

## Construction and census

The construction uses the frozen Cycle 41 singleton marginals and pair
transports. Each of the 11,852 unordered face classes is solved once in sorted
type order and reused at every occurrence; repeated labels are averaged over
their stabilizer. The resulting 11,926 nonzero face coefficients induce
10,516 common oriented pair moments.

For each of the 3,954 raw four-type interfaces, the standard alternating sum
of its four shared face tensors is checked to have zero simplicial boundary.
Exact rational elimination against allowed tetrahedra fills every cycle. All
coefficients are integral with magnitude one. Face support is one in 11,839
classes, three in one class, and seven in twelve classes. Tetrahedral fill
support is one on 3,942 interfaces and seven on twelve interfaces; the cycle
supports are four, ten, or twelve.

The preregistered coherent affine fallback was not run because the canonical
census had no failure. Its absence is an outcome-rule consequence, not a
dropped negative branch.

## Verification

The primary route reconstructs raw rank-two and induced deletion data and
uses lowest-pivot exact elimination. It serializes every shared face tensor,
every oriented moment cycle, and every tetrahedral fill.

The independent route derives blocker membership directly from type/signature
incidence in reverse order. It checks rank-one, rank-two, rank-three, induced,
and binary-mediated forced-zero support; all singleton and shared pair
marginals; repeated-type symmetry; all 11,926 face coefficients; all 15,890
cycle coefficients; and all 4,026 fill coefficients. It independently
reconstructs the 409 structural complexes and verifies every boundary.

## Structural clue, not theorem

The twelve seven-term cases have the signed cube-with-one-corner pattern, and
their four-way fills are corresponding seven-term cones. The remaining cases
are single tetrahedra. This suggests a cone or discrete-Morse contraction, but
Cycle 43 does not prove such an operator exists outside the three anchor
families. The sparse integral pattern is `OBSERVED` structural evidence inside
the exact `PROVED` finite census.

## Claim boundary

The three anchors were selected from Cycle 41's nonzero-H1 boundary. Cycle 43
does not cover a stratified non-anchor sample or all four-type tuples, does not
prove a natural contraction, does not span all rank-three-literal multiplier
constraints, and is not a full degree-four functional, a leaf certificate, or
LRC(13).

# C93 canonical LEM amalgam boundary

`PROVED`: the one frozen 16-vertex free amalgam has 303,908,928 linear
extensions. Forward-ideal and maximal-deletion DPs agree on the total and all
six marked pair counts. Transitive closure makes the second tip comparable to
the shared triangle vertices; the required incomparabilities and marked
ordered-two-triangle arrows therefore fail, and no full ordered-tip cycle is
present.

## Claim boundary

This refutes only the canonical two-copy, shared-\(0\to3\), \(x<y\) free
amalgam. It says nothing about other amalgams, inverse constructions, the
realizability of the full ordered two-triangle configuration, or Gupta
Question 14.

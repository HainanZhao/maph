## Claim boundary

These controls neither prove nor disprove (C5-K) for arbitrary graphons or
completely-positive kernels, and say nothing decisive about Sidorenko for
\(K_{5,5}\setminus C_{10}\).  The nonnegative finite packet is not evidence
authorizing a larger grid.  Under C85's frozen pivot rule, this closes only
the bounded C5-kernel method attempt.

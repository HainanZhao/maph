# Equality consequences for the high-star defect bound

Assume the hypotheses of the high-star lemma and \(D(H)=5\). Then every
inequality in

\[
D(H)\ge\sum_{r\ne v}\binom{k_r}{2}\ge |R|\ge5
\]

is equality. Hence \(|R|=5\), every \(r\in R\) lies on exactly two star
lines, and no pair of lines outside the star—or star pair beyond these five
vertices—has an excess intersection.

For every \(r\in R\), the five vertices \(\{v\}\cup(R\setminus\{r\})\)
cannot cover \(H\).  Since every non-star line meets \(R\), there is a
non-star line whose trace on \(R\) is exactly \(\{r\}\). Thus the trace
hypergraph \(\{L\cap R:v\notin L\}\) contains every singleton on its
five-point ground set and has transversal number five.

This rigid equality design is the sole C71 equality audit. A realization
shows the defect bound is sharp; an exact contradiction upgrades it to
\(D(H)\ge6\). Neither outcome authorizes an automatic higher-defect ladder.

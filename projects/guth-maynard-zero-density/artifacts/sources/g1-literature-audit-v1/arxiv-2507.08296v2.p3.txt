Zero density estimates for the Riemann zeta function have important consequences
for the distribution of primes. Similarly, zero density estimates for Dirichlet L-functions
have important consequences for the distribution of primes in arithmetic progressions,
particularly in short intervals. In contrast to the Riemann zeta function, a key difficulty
for Dirichlet L-functions is the lack of a sufficiently strong zero-free region in the q-
aspect. In particular, there is the possibility of an exceptional real and simple zero

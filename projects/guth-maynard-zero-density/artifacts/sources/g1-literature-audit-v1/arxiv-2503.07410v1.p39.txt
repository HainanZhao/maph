By considering the special examples above, Bourgain showed that Conjecture 3.4 implies Con-
jecture 9.3 and hence (37). He also showed that these conjectures imply Conjecture 9.1, the original
Kakeya conjecture for tubes. Because of Bourgain’s work introducing this problem, I propose to
call this question the Bourgain-Kakeya problem and to call Conjecture 9.3 the Bourgain-Kakeya
conjecture.

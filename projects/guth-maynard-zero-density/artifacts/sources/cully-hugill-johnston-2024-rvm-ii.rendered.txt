ON THE ERROR TERM IN THE EXPLICIT FORMULA OF
RIEMANN–VON MANGOLDT II

MICHAELA CULLY-HUGILL AND DANIEL R. JOHNSTON

Abstract. We give an explicit O(x/T) error term for the truncated Riemann–
von Mangoldt explicit formula. For large x, this provides a modest improve-
ment over previous work, which we demonstrate via an application to a result
on primes between consecutive powers.

1. Introduction

This paper gives a new explicit estimate for the error in the truncated Riemann–
von Mangoldt explicit formula. This is a sequel to [4], which gave a related result.
In contrast to [4], this new estimate is asymptotically sharper, but allows for less
control over the point of truncation.

Although we now have less control over the point of truncation, for most ap-

moderate values of x the best estimate will often come from using some ω < 1.

avenues.

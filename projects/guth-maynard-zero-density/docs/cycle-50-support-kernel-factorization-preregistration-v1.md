# Cycle 50 preregistration: support-kernel factorization

## Question

For `F_(m,s)=K(t)^sK(mt)`, replace the opaque coefficient-ratio measure by
an exact support correlation when `m>s`, then write the corresponding
Halász--Montgomery large-value inequality.

## Frozen tasks

1. Prove that for `m>s` the label `q^m p_1...p_s` uniquely determines `q`
   and the unordered ordinary-prime multiset.
2. Express the support polynomial as `K(mt) h_s`, where `h_s` is the complete
   homogeneous symmetric polynomial in the prime phases.
3. Derive exact power-sum formulas for `h_3` and `h_4` and verify them by
   exhaustive finite alphabets of sizes one through four.
4. State the phase-aligned Halász--Montgomery inequality for any row subset
   on which `|F_(m,s)|>=V_m`, retaining the exact off-diagonal kernel.
5. Separate the finitely many orders `2<=m<=s`; no formula for those orders
   may be inferred from the injective case.

## Claim boundary

This cycle may prove an exact factorization and reduction. It may not infer a
bound for the factored difference kernel, `AMPR_s`, `LCAM_s`, density, or
prime intervals without the new difference-set estimate.

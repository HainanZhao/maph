# Cycle 41 annular sampling preregistration v1

## Claim boundary

This cycle may prove a smooth sampling reduction from hollow separated
values to annular mean values and derive the exact signed Fourier kernel of
the annulus. It may not promote the required annular mean bound, a kernel
count, a density gain, or an interval gain.

## Frozen tasks

1. Center `F_(m,s)` and use a smooth reproducing kernel at bandwidth
   `B_m=O(s+m)`.
2. Sum the reproducing inequality over a `Delta`-separated hollow row set.
3. Bound leakage from the zero neighborhood and exterior tails using an
   explicitly registered Schwartz decay order `N=9`.
4. Express the remaining annular integral as a signed collision form and
   compute the sufficient vector target.
5. Keep the new annular arithmetic estimate tagged `CONJECTURED` unless it
   is independently proved.

Hostile audit remains deferred to paper stage.

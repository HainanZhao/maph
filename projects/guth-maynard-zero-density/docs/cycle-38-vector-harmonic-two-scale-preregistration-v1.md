# Cycle 38 vector-harmonic two-scale preregistration v1

## Claim boundary

This cycle may prove exact rescaling-collision, unique-factorization, norm,
and exponent ledgers for the vector harmonic route. It may not promote a
large-values, kernel-count, density, or interval gain without a new analytic
bound with strict margin.

## Frozen scale

```text
H=X^(12/5), Delta=X^(3/5), V=X^(7/10),
A=X^a, 0<a<=3/10, M=X^(1+o(1)).
```

The harmonic vector is `(K(mt))_(2<=m<=A)`. Flattening `(t,m)` to the
rescaled ordinate `u=mt` is permitted only after its collision multiplicity
is computed.

## Registered tasks

1. Construct or exclude a `Delta`-separated fan with `A` distinct pairs
   `(t_m,m)` sharing one rescaled ordinate.
2. Compute the expanded height `AH` and fixed-`m` spacing.
3. Retain the original large value by defining

   ```text
   D_m(t)=K(t)K(mt)=sum_(p,q)(p q^m)^(-it).
   ```

   For every integer `m>=2`, prove or refute injectivity of
   `(p,q)->p q^m`, including the diagonal `p=q`, and compute the exact
   coefficient-square norm.
4. If a row has excess harmonic energy
   `sum_m|K(mt)/M|^2>=E`, compute its forced two-scale energy
   `sum_m|D_m(t)|^2`.
5. State the new vector-valued analytic theorem required without replacing
   ambient length by coefficient cardinality unless proved.

## Outcomes

- `VECTOR_GAIN` only if the full vector avoids a fixed-power collision and
  yields a checked analytic saving.
- `TWO_SCALE_REDUCTION` if flattening is sharp but retaining `K(t)` produces
  an injective prime-monomial family.
- `VECTOR_SATURATION` if even the two-scale family has a sharp admissible
  countermodel at the target.

Hostile audit remains deferred to paper stage.

# Cycle 52 preregistration: support-kernel self-duality

## Question

Extract the top distinct-prime stratum from the Cycle 51 all-harmonic support
polynomial and quantify what a large support correlation forces for
`K(h)` and `K(mh)`.

## Frozen tasks

1. Prove uniformly for fixed `s` and every `m>=2` that
   `G_(m,s)(h)=K(mh)K(h)^s/s!+O_s(M^s)`.
2. Check the count of collision tuples and lower-support partitions without
   allowing constants to depend on growing `m`.
3. If `|G_(m,s)(h)|>=M^(s+1)X^(-eta)` with fixed `eta<1`, derive exact
   deficit inequalities for `|K(h)|` and `|K(mh)|`.
4. Insert the factorization into the Cycle 50 Halász--Montgomery inequality
   and state the resulting difference-set recurrence gate.

## Claim boundary

This cycle may prove exact leading-stratum asymptotics and an inverse
implication. It may not infer that enough differences are large, apply a
Balog--Szemerédi theorem without its quantitative hypotheses, or promote
`ADK_s`, `AMPR_s`, `LCAM_s`, density, or interval results.

# Cycle 67 preregistration: seeded deep-packet recurrence

## Question

Determine exactly what additional hypothesis turns a deep beta-free packet
into realized arithmetic-progression recurrence in the original transport
census.

## Frozen setup

- A seed satisfies `|j0+beta-h0 alpha|<=C0/X` with `h0 in [H,2H]`.
- A primitive packet satisfies `(a,q)=1`, `qK<=H`, and
  `|q alpha-a|<=C1/(KX)`.
- Only the strip constant may enlarge; no power of `X` may be lost.

## Outcomes

- `SEEDED_AP_RECURRENCE`: prove that one seed creates `gg K` genuine hits
  on one of the progressions `h0+kq`, with an explicit enlarged strip
  constant, and identify the critical exponent interface.
- `BETA_FREE_ONLY`: packet depth supplies possible differences but cannot be
  converted into actual transport hits even after one seed is supplied.

No estimate for the number of seeds or packets, large-value theorem, powered
saving, density gain, or interval gain is asserted by
`SEEDED_AP_RECURRENCE`.

Date frozen: 2026-08-02 UTC.

Poisson-sum the Cycle-121 radial profile in the actual integer variable `n`,
where `A=p0n` and the reduced anchor is bounded. Include all corrected smooth
cutoffs. Prove rapid decay of the zero Poisson mode from the vanishing of
every derivative of the dyadic cutoff `U` at zero, after accounting for the
nonlinear map `y=c log(p0n/S)` and smooth support edges.

For every nonzero Poisson mode, derive the exact stationary point, phase,
curvature, amplitude, and support order. Success is a volume-free alias
operator with `ell~K`; it is not a bound for the nonzero aliases, a
simple-root theorem, or moment closure.

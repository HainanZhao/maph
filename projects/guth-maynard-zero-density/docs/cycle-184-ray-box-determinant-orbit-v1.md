# Cycle 184: LCM resonance and nonrational ray-box deformation

## Claim boundary

`PROVED`: the exact primitive determinant converts the two ray-orbit errors
to an LCM resonance, but introduces no independent approximation error.
`PROVED`: a scale-matched two-label actual positive exponential can have a
common-intercept primitive-ray box with a nonrational phase, stable nonzero
determinant, complete fibres, and all fibre depths below the critical seed
scale. Its largest frozen box has only `X^(11/25-o(1))` rectangles.

This is an `LCM_RESONANCE_REDUNDANCY` no-go only. It proves no upper bound

for that box, no aggregate recurrence, density gain, or prime-interval result.

## LCM resonance

Let `U=v*u`, `V=v*w`, `g=gcd(u,w)`, and set

```text
epsilon_l=A-U*alpha_l,       epsilon_m=B-V*alpha_m,
F=w*A-u*B,                  L=lcm(U,V)=v*u*w/g.
```

Then `g|F` and direct substitution gives

```text
F/g-L*(alpha_l-alpha_m)
 = (w/g)*epsilon_l-(u/g)*epsilon_m.                         (1)
```

Consequently the C183 orbit widths imply the LCM resonance with the weighted
sum of their two errors. Equation (1) is useful state compression, but it is
not an additional small quantity: it is exactly generated by the two original
approximations. A census saving therefore needs an actual-exponential
distribution theorem for the varying LCMs.

## A nonrational actual exponential deformation

For every sufficiently large odd integer `T`, put

```text
X=T^50, H=T^22, V=T^11, U=V^2, n=T^30, M=T^80,
B=(3V+1)/2, q=B/V,
r=sqrt(q^2+1/M), z=r^(1/n), Delta=2*pi*n/log(r).
```

Thus `alpha_n=r` and `alpha_(2n)=r^2` lie on one actual positive
exponential. After reduction,

```text
r^2=(B^2*T^58+1)/T^80,
```

whose numerator is `(B*T^29)^2+1`, so `r` is nonrational. At the two labels
use the full integral fibres on `j=(B/V)h` and `j=(B^2/V^2)h`, with
`beta=rho=0`. The residuals are below `1/X`, their depths are
`T^11+1` and `2`, and both are below `X^(6/25)=T^12`.

Their primitive determinant is

```text
F=V*B*(V-B) != 0,
```

and the product shell is stable. The two labels lie in the inherited
`c=1/2` chart. All physical pairs are stable; partitioning the left pair
multiplier by its frozen dyadic bins leaves one box with
`X^(11/25-o(1))` rectangles. This is far below the critical packet scale.

## Gate effect

The C184 candidate engine closes only an LCM-only compression attempt. The
next engine must use population across a varying family of labels/denominators
or additional actual-exponential structure—preferably three-label/shared-
fibre compatibility or cross-box coefficient entropy. Neither `gcd(u,w)|F`
nor a single LCM resonance can by itself force phase rationality or a critical
seed; this does not rule out a population-sensitive critical-box theorem.

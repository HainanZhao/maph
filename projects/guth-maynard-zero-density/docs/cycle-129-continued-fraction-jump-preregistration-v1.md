# Cycle 129 preregistration: continued-fraction jump compiler

Date frozen: 2026-08-02 UTC.

For a Cycle-92 collision on dyadic multiplicity scale `M`, write its reduced
label as `p/q` and retain the bounds

```text
|p/q-g^a|<<1/(KQ),  q<<Q/M.
```

Prove Legendre's criterion applies uniformly in the full lower band. If
`p/q` is the resulting convergent, apply the elementary two-sided error bound
in terms of the next convergent denominator to force lower bounds for that
denominator and the next partial quotient. Freeze all exponent margins.

Success is a deterministic large-partial-quotient inverse and the exact
averaged counting theorem that would close each multiplicity class. It is not
itself such an averaged theorem or a collision bound.

# Cycle 131: order three extends the closed denominator range

Let `q~X^rho` inside a Cycle-127 multiplicity class `M=X^mu`. Applying the
checked order-three Huxley--Sargos theorem in the numerator, summing the
`X^rho` denominators, and restoring `M` gives

```text
derivative: mu+1/10+3rho/2,
tube:       mu+2rho+4/45-xi/3,
ratio:      mu+2rho-xi/3-1/9,
constant:   mu+rho.                               (1)
```

The target exponent is `1/3`. The derivative term reaches it at

```text
rho_HS=7/45-2mu/3.                                (2)
```

At (2), the margins for the other three terms are respectively

```text
(xi+mu)/3-1/15,
2/15+(xi+mu)/3,
8/45-mu/3.                                        (3)
```

All are positive throughout the registered lower band and low-multiplicity
range. Therefore every block with `rho<=rho_HS` meets the `QX^epsilon`
collision target.

This strictly extends Cycle 130. Its excess over the broad-cylinder ceiling
is

```text
xi/2+mu/3-14/45 >=2/225.                          (4)
```

The full denominator ceiling is `1/3-mu`, so the sole unresolved endpoint
range is

```text
7/45-2mu/3 < rho <= 1/3-mu.                       (5)
```

Its width is

```text
8/45-mu/3 >=133/900.                              (6)
```

even though its denominatorwise sum over the entire range failed in Cycle
127. No estimate for (5), full low-multiplicity or simple-root closure,

# Cycle 45 joint `(p,k)` large-sieve preregistration v1

## Claim boundary

This cycle may prove the wrap-coloring geometry of the curved Beatty
frequencies, apply the classical separated-frequency large sieve, and compute
the resulting joint-sum exponent. It may define a de-aliasing target, but may
not promote that target, a prime-pair estimate, `LCAM_s`, density, or interval
gain without the missing theorem.

# Cycle 37 excess-harmonic routing preregistration v1

## Claim boundary

This cycle may prove finite cyclic Fourier/KL identities and abstract
histogram countermodels for entropy-excess routing. It may not assert that an
abstract histogram is realized by actual prime phases, nor promote a kernel,
density, or interval gain.

## Frozen model

Use `L` equally spaced circle points with uniform mass `u_j=1/L`. Let
`qstar` be a positive first-harmonic information projection with prescribed
Fourier coefficient `r`. Define `E(q)=D(q||qstar)` for histograms `q` with
the same first coefficient.

## Registered questions

1. Relate `E(q)` to a weighted chi-square divergence and the total non-first
   Fourier mass.
2. Construct, for every registered harmonic `2<=m<=L-2`, a positive
   perturbation of `qstar` that preserves total mass and first Fourier
   coefficient exactly while placing its new Fourier mass at `m`.
3. Compute the unavoidable pigeonhole loss when only the existence of one
   harmonic among `L=X^(3/10+o(1))` choices is known.
4. Determine whether raw entropy excess selects a bounded harmonic. If not,
   pivot to joint prime-row coupling or a vector-valued estimate over all
   harmonics.

## Outcomes

- `BOUNDED_HARMONIC` if KL excess forces a controlled fixed/subpower order.
- `HARMONIC_HIDING` if an exact abstract family hides excess at arbitrary
  registered order while preserving the first moment.
- `CORRECTION` if the Cycle 36 excess decomposition is inconsistent.

Hostile audit remains deferred to paper stage.

# Cycle 51 preregistration: all-harmonic support partitions

## Question

Remove Cycle 50's exceptional orders `2<=m<=s` by classifying the distinct
prime-exponent partitions in the support of `K(t)^sK(mt)`.

## Frozen tasks

1. Prove the support criterion: a partition `lambda` of `s+m` occurs iff its
   largest part is at least `m`.
2. Enumerate the admitted partitions for `(s,m)` equal to
   `(3,2),(3,3),(4,2),(4,3),(4,4)`.
3. Express every monomial symmetric function by exact Möbius inversion over
   set partitions, hence as a polynomial in power sums `K(jh)`.
4. Verify the power-sum expression against direct finite-alphabet evaluation
   for registered alphabets of sizes one through four.
5. Reconcile the `m>s` specialization with `K(mh)H_s(h)`.

## Claim boundary

This cycle may prove the support-kernel classification for all `m>=2`. It
may not claim a bound for the resulting kernel polynomial, `DK_s`,
`AMPR_s`, `LCAM_s`, density, or prime intervals.

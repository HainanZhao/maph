## P1R-FS: fixed-splice architecture

Freeze (s_0=7/10) and

\[
I(\sigma)=\frac3{2-\sigma}\qquad(1/2\le\sigma<s_0).
\]

Only the right side [(s_0,1)] may be replaced. The required exact
two-route target is

\[
\frac{30}{13}-I(\sigma)=
\frac{30(7/10-\sigma)}{13(2-\sigma)},\qquad
\sup_{1/2\le\sigma<7/10}I(\sigma)=\frac{30}{13}.
\]

`PROVED`, once the separately required two routes are completed: within this
formal fixed-splice class, a right-only replacement cannot certify a strict
uniform coefficient below (30/13). This does not assert a lower bound for
the actual zero count, saturation of the Guth--Maynard method, or an
obstruction to a left-neighborhood improvement.

FS falsifiers are a pinned-source/range mismatch, a failed exact identity, or
a failed strict-supremum witness. A proof which alters the left branch or its
splice lies outside the frozen class rather than refuting it.

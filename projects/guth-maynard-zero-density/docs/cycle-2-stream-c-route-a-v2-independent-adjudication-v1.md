## Outcome

`PROVED`: Cully-Hugill--Johnston I, Theorem 1.2, is a fixed-height formula
only for

\[
 \max\{51,\log z\}<T<(z^\alpha-2)/2,\qquad 0<\alpha\leq\tfrac12.
\]

Thus it covers the eventual *uniform* Route-A choice at both endpoints:

\[
 T_u=x/y\,\exp(2(\log x)^{1/4})
 \leq x^{13/30-\epsilon}\exp(2(\log x)^{1/4})=o(x^{1/2})
\]

when \(y\geq x^{17/30+\epsilon}\). The lower bound is also eventual, and
the upper bound at \(x+y\) follows from the one at \(x\), because the CHJ-I
upper envelope increases with its argument. This is only a subbranch result.

`PROVED`: it does **not** cover the Route-A almost-all choice. Guth--Maynard
set

\[
 \delta=X^{-13/15+\epsilon/2},\qquad
 T_a=\delta^{-1}\exp(4(\log X)^{1/4})
   =X^{13/15-\epsilon/2}\exp(4(\log X)^{1/4}).
\]

For every \(0<\epsilon<11/15\), its power exponent exceeds \(1/2\). Hence
\(T_a/X^{1/2}\to\infty\), and replacing \(X\) by either endpoint in
\([X,3X]\) changes only a constant. CHJ-I explicitly says that it cannot take
\(\alpha>1/2\). Its theorem therefore cannot, by itself, justify Route-A v2's
prescribed-height formula in the almost-all argument.

`OBSERVED`: the Route-A-v2 source-level PASS/promotion is not licensed as a
whole-route conclusion. The specific failed containment is the almost-all
formula node; no statement here retracts the uniform subbranch.

`OBSERVED`: Route-A's v2 JSON is regenerated by its historical test and embeds
`wall_time_ns`; its byte hash consequently changes on replay. This is a
reproducibility-design defect, not unexplained concurrent content mutation.
This adjudication therefore pins its stable `exact_replay_sha256` semantic
identity and records the observed post-writer byte hash separately. The

CHJ-I alone closes the prescribed-height formula.

## Endpoint and convention audit

`PROVED`: CHJ-I defines \(\psi(z)=\sum_{n\leq z}\Lambda(n)\). Subtracting
the theorem at the endpoints initially represents \((x,x+y]\), whereas
Guth--Maynard prints \([x,x+y]\). The difference is at most the contribution
of an integral endpoint and is \(O(\log x)\); this must be stated, rather
than hidden under “endpoint conventions.”

`PROVED`: CHJ-I's actual displayed truncation is \(|\gamma|\leq T\), while
Guth--Maynard prints \(|\rho|\leq T\). If the latter is literal complex
modulus rather than notation for ordinate height, the difference lies in a
unit-width boundary strip and is not an identity of sums.

`OBSERVED`: CHJ-I says “all non-trivial zeros,” but its frozen source does
not explicitly state a multiplicity convention. Ford's convention cannot be
silently imported into another source. This is insufficient for a standalone
CHJ-I convention closure.

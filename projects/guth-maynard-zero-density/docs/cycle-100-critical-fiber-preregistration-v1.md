# Cycle 100 preregistration: oriented critical-fiber atlas

## Claim boundary

This cycle may derive the exact unsigned fiber count, its gcd factorization,
and a generic-sector bound. It may not claim a bound for the cross-valuation
web, import an unproved Möbius sign, close weak near-double rows, prove the
alias moment, or promote a density/interval gain.

## Frozen notation

- `w` is a fixed nonzero signed mode difference and `W=|w|>=2`.
- The sign orientation is determined by `w`; put `s=|a|`, `t=|b|`, so
  `s+t=W` and `1<=s,t<=W-1`.
- `N,R` are coprime positive integers giving the reduced critical label.
- `1<=B,C<=Q` and the exact fiber equation is `CtR=BsN`.
- No Möbius weight is attached to `(B,C,s,t)` in this atlas. Any later signed
  sum must reinsert the frozen stationary phases/amplitudes from its source.

## Preregistered identities and gates

1. For each split, put `g=gcd(sN,tR)` and prove all solutions are

```text
B=lambda*tR/g, C=lambda*sN/g,
1<=lambda<=Q*g/max(sN,tR).
```

2. Prove the exact fiber count

```text
F=sum_(s=1)^(W-1) floor(Q*gcd(sN,(W-s)R)/max(sN,(W-s)R)).
```

3. Put `g0=gcd(s,t)` and prove

```text
gcd(sN,tR)=g0*gcd(s/g0,R)*gcd(t/g0,N).
```

4. Define the generic sector by both cross gcds being one. Prove

```text
F_generic<=2Q*tau(W)/min(N,R).
```

5. Define every nongeneric split as the explicit cross-valuation web
`gcd(s/g0,R)>1` or `gcd(t/g0,N)>1`, retaining which side and prime powers.

## Falsifiers

- An enumerated positive solution not generated by the lambda formula halts
  the atlas.
- Failure of the exact gcd product on any integer row halts the valuation
  split.
- Any cancellation claim without the original phase/amplitude formula is
  forbidden.

# Cycle 132 preregistration: unimodular endpoint lift

Date frozen: 2026-08-02 UTC.

For every Cycle-131 endpoint collision, attach to the reduced convergent
`p/q` its next convergent `P/R`, retaining the orientation
`s=Pq-pR in {+1,-1}`.  Dyadically restrict `q~X^rho` and `R~X^tau`.

Use the elementary consecutive-convergent error bounds and the congruence
`pR=-s (mod q)` to combine all allowed `R` in one residue class.  Determine
the resulting interval width in the logarithmic mode coordinate, its natural
Fourier bandwidth, and the full volume exponent after restoring the
multiplicity `M=X^mu`.

Then state the exact clustered large-sieve norm which would close the block.
Success is either:

1. a strict closed endpoint subrange; or
2. a proved dichotomy in which failure of the target produces clusters of
   rational labels carrying their consecutive unimodular matrices, with an
   explicit tolerance compatible with the Cycle-125 ray-web compiler.

No generic equidistribution of logarithms modulo one may be assumed.  A
volume calculation alone is not endpoint closure.

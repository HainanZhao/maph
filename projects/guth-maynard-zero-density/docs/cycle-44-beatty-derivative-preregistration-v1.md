# Cycle 44 Beatty-literature and derivative-test preregistration v1

## Claim boundary

This cycle may check exact published hypotheses for fixed-slope Beatty-prime
results and the explicit van der Corput derivative theorem, then compute the
available exponent saving for the Cycle 43 slope family. It may delimit only
those cited results, not all possible Beatty or exponential-sum methods.

## Registered tasks

1. Check whether cited Beatty-prime theorems allow an `X`-dependent slope
   tending to zero and whether their prime-pair conclusions are unconditional.
2. Apply the explicit `d`-th derivative theorem to
   `f(x)=h p(exp(2pi x/Delta)-1)` with `h=X^nu`.
3. Compute the three theorem terms for every `d>=3` at the required
   resolution `nu=11/25`.
4. Identify the best saving supplied by this theorem family and compare it
   with both Cycle 39 margins.

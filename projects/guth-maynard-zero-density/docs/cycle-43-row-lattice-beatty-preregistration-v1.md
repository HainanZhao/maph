# Cycle 43 row-lattice/Beatty-strip preregistration v1

## Claim boundary

This cycle may analyze the arithmetic-progression row stress model for
`LCAM_s`, derive its exact resonance window, and translate one-prime
coefficient replacements into curved prime-pair strips. It may not claim
that arbitrary separated rows reduce to progressions or that the required
prime-pair estimate is proved.

## Registered tasks

1. Compute the row Fourier sum for `C={j Delta:1<=j<=R}`.
2. Give an explicit sufficient window for full-size Dirichlet-kernel
   resonance around `2pi k/Delta`.
3. For `xi=log((p+r)/p)`, translate that window without linearizing the
   exponential center.
4. Freeze the target exponents at `R=X^(21/25)` and record whether the strip
   contains more than one integer shift.
5. Compare the exact exponential center with its first-order approximation.

  contradiction, may be the useful theorem.

  smaller scalar density coefficient if the same transport geometry forces

Creative rule: new transforms, calculi, inequalities, and computational

kills that formulation only; the broader engine remains live unless a proved

- F1: an actual zero-derived coefficient/set family simultaneously saturates
  the polyphonic detector, centred interaction, and transport-curvature

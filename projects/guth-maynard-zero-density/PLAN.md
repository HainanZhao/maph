1. prove, for explicit `eta>0`, a uniform estimate

for all automorphic L-functions, and promotion of empirical optimization as
proof.

### 7.1 E1 — polyphonic zero detection

The classical reduction asks one capped coefficient vector to be large on
every retained zero ordinate. E1 replaces it by a structured weighted frame
`b^(1),...,b^(K)` and its mixed PSD kernel, not mere colour partitioning.

- `PROVED` toy theorem: the frame kernel obeys the exact trace inequality and
  a uniform colour cover pays `K^r` at moment `r`. The existing `O(log T)`
  dyadic zero-detector choice is already subpower and yields no gain by mere
  colouring or pigeonholing.
- `PROVED` hybrid boundary: a block frame is `dd*/K+Z` with `Z>=0`. Raw
  Schatten traces retain `dd*/K` at full exponent, while detector largeness
  alone does not force `Z`. E1 is now an arithmetic variance problem.
- `CONJECTURED` lever: a prime-block or factorization-derived frame may have
  mixed traces smaller than unrelated scalar traces by more than the explicit
  colour loss, even when no single vector has good all-row flatness.
- Target mechanism: replace the max-min statistic `Gamma` by a low-rank or
  nuclear-norm statistic and couple colours through mixed Gram traces.
- Kill test: `PROVED` lower bounds forcing `K>=v^kappa` together with a sharp
  colour cost that cancels every prospective saving. A failed numerical
  dictionary search is only `OBSERVED` and does not kill E1.

### 7.2 E2 — centred nonbacktracking trace

Guth--Maynard already subtract the scalar cubic spectral mean. E2 instead
deletes exact row-local two-step returns and retains a positive square.

- `PROVED` toy theorem: with `A=G-dI`, `r_i=sum_j|A_ij|^2`, and
  `C_2=A^2-diag(r)`, one has
  `||C_2||_F^2=tr(A^4)-sum_i r_i^2` and a one-sided spectral bound. Raw
  length-four nonbacktracking trace is signed, so it is not the engine.
- `CONJECTURED` lever: source-derived frame diversity or alias-conditional
  centring lowers both the maximal `r_i` and `C_2` on Base-derived row sets.
- `PROVED` hybrid boundary: the rank-one detector component itself realizes
  the coherent `C_2` scale, so E2 must be applied after that component is
  removed or to a genuinely different ensemble.
- Target mechanism: replace `PositiveCubic` by a centred excess estimate, or
  show that a large uncentred cubic forces a structured resonant component
  that can be routed into E3.
- Kill test: an actual Base-compatible family whose centred nonbacktracking
  spectral radius remains at the trivial exponent. Positivity failure is a
  design constraint, not a route death: matrix dilation, even trace, or a
  sum-of-squares certificate remains authorized.

### 7.3 E3 — logarithmic-Farey curvature and inverse rigidity

The present energy bound treats `log n` almost as arbitrary frequencies and
Farey cells almost as arbitrary close pairs. E3 retains the curvature of
`u -> exp(u)`, unique factorization of the integer frequencies, and the
reduced-rational labels simultaneously.

- `CONJECTURED` lever: near equality in the actual-log Farey fourth moment
  forces an approximate phase lattice; near Base saturation forces the same
  coefficients to behave like an approximate multiplicative character.
  These two structures should be quantitatively incompatible, except for a
  classifiable family.
- First toy theorem: classify `v^-o(1)` near-extremizers in a two-block model
  (two separated prime ranges and one Farey denominator scale). The desired
  conclusion is either entropy growth across blocks or concentration on a
  bounded-rank multiplicative progression.
- Target mechanism: a coefficient-sensitive inverse theorem followed by a
  sum-product, S-unit, or decoupling estimate on the surviving structured
  case. The theorem must use the actual logarithms; generic alias models are
  already known to be sharp.
- Kill test: construct an actual integer-frequency, capped-coefficient family
  simultaneously realizing phase-lattice energy, Base scale, and the
  rational-mass geometry. Such a construction is itself a publishable
  extremizer theorem.

Starting from a density profile `C_0(sigma)`, use it inside the exceptional-set
and detector construction to produce `C_1=Phi(C_0)`, and seek a strict
subsolution near the crossover. E6 bypasses scalar density when possible by
grouping zero contributions in the explicit formula according to the same
resonant geometry exposed by E2--E4.

- `CONJECTURED` E5 lever: a small local saving can improve the exceptional-set
  input, which enlarges the next saving and changes the left branch instead
  of merely splicing a better right branch onto unchanged Ingham.
- `CONJECTURED` E6 lever: uniform short-interval estimates may need a bound
  for a weighted complex sum over zeros, not the absolute count of all zeros;
  centred clusters may cancel even when scalar density is saturated.
- First E5 toy theorem: write the exact monotone map `Phi` for a simplified
  two-cell detector and determine its fixed points symbolically. First E6 toy
  theorem: obtain a deterministic cluster estimate stronger than triangle
  inequality under one source-checked spacing or phase hypothesis.
- Kill tests: `Phi` has a rigorously attracting fixed point `30/13` for every
  admissible feedback input; or a coherent zero model meeting all proved
  constraints saturates the direct explicit-formula bound at `17/30`.

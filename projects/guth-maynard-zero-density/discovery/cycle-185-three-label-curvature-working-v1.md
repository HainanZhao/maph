## Derivation A: deep arithmetic-progression exactification (CONJECTURED)

Write `a_i=A_i/U_i=alpha_i+delta_i` for the labels
`i in {-,0,+}`, where the outer labels are `ell-r,ell+r`, and retain

```text
|delta_i| <= 2C/((N_i-1)*U_i*X),
alpha_-*alpha_+ = alpha_0^2,
K=U_0^2*A_-*A_+ - U_-*U_+*A_0^2.
```

After division by `P=U_-*U_+*U_0^2`, the main exponential term cancels:

```text
K/P = alpha_-*delta_+ + alpha_+*delta_- + delta_-*delta_+
      -2*alpha_0*delta_0 - delta_0^2.                     (A1)
```

On the inherited chart all `alpha_i` are bounded by `A_c=exp(2*pi*c)`.
If all `N_i-1>=S`, C182 capacity gives `U_i<=H/S`; hence the linear part of
`|K|` is at most

```text
8*A_c*C*H^3/(S^4*X),                                      (A2)
```

and the quadratic part is smaller once `S>=1` and `H<X`. Thus a fixed
constant multiple of

```text
S_3=(H^3/X)^(1/4)=X^(2/25)                                (A3)
```

forces `|K|<1`, hence the exact rational geometric relation
`a_-*a_+=a_0^2`.

This is a new three-label exactification threshold; it is not an LCM identity
generated by two orbit errors. It has not yet produced a census saving.

## Derivation B: common-intercept divisibility lift (CONJECTURED)

In a fixed packet write `U_i=v*u_i`. The curvature has the exact factor

```text
K=v^2*K',
K'=u_0^2*A_-*A_+ - u_-*u_+*A_0^2.                         (D1)
```

Moreover, with the C183 primitive pair determinants

```text
F_-0=u_0*A_- - u_-*A_0,
F_0+=u_+*A_0 - u_0*A_+,
```

there is a retained physical-coefficient syzygy

```text
K' = u_0*A_+*F_-0 - u_-*A_0*F_0+.                         (D2)
```

Expanding (B1) directly against the three orbit errors and substituting
`u_i<=H/(v*S)` improves (A2) to

```text
|K'| <= 8*A_c*C*H^3/(v^2*S^4*X) + lower-order quadratic terms. (D3)
```

Thus depths above a constant multiple of
`S_(3,v)=(H^3/(v^2*X))^(1/4)=X^(2/25)/sqrt(v)` exactify the
*primitive* curvature. The lift is genuinely available only because C181
fixed one common intercept before C183 cleared the rays. It is still not a
population theorem: the `v=1` AP-free occupancy design remains legal at the
level of mass and capacity.

## Population obstruction (CONJECTURED)

The critical packet lower bound alone need not force a dense set of labels
above `S_3`: pair mass `P_pair~X^(21/50)` can in principle be supplied by
about `X^(13/50)` labels of depth `S_3`, far below the label ambient scale
`Delta=X^(15/25)`. Ordinary dense three-AP theorems therefore do not apply
directly. The next check is whether the *weighted*, full-fibre rectangle
state forces enough arithmetic-progression triples, or whether a sparse
high-depth construction survives with all multipliers and stable shells.

ordered cross-label rectangle mass is

```text

```

`r*U^2>=X^(18/25)`, well above the `X^(1/25)` stable threshold. Standard
large three-AP-free subsets of `[1,Delta]` contain such an `M`-element set,

applies.

simultaneous rational-slope approximations. It does prove that a successful
C185 argument must exploit the actual distribution of approximants or a
coefficient relation across boxes; weighted population and fibre capacity
alone cannot force the needed arithmetic progression.

# Cycle 95 discovery candidate: exact entropy modes are central

`CONJECTURED`: after Poisson summation in `(h,Delta)`, let the integer modes
be `(u,v)` and put `g=exp(2pi/D)`. The stationary equations of the Cycle-94
entropy phase should combine to

```text
c0 n-c0 n' g^u-m g^(u+v)=0.
```

For rational `c0=p0/q0`, this is a Laurent polynomial over the integers in
`g`. Gelfond--Schneider implies `g` is transcendental. Positivity should then
force `u=v=0` and `p0(n-n')=q0m`.

This note is discovery only. It says nothing quantitative about near-zero
Laurent polynomials.

three-coordinate divisor web: source core, numerator absorption, and

## Rejection rule

A result that merely shows a large unweighted gcd, loses beta/seed/range
labels, or assumes that a content moment itself supplies compatible mass is
discarded. The registered countermodel is a bounded two-level eligible bank
that saturates the sharp moment-to-population inequality. The companion
approved the seal after the exhaustive signed factorization check corrected
the false claim that the source-core factor is coprime to both edge factors.
Next work must prove `M>W` for actual complete eligible pairs or
quantitatively bank seed/range, error, capacity, source-core, numerator, or
denominator mass.

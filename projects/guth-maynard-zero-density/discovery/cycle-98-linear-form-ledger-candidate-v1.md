# Cycle 98 discovery candidate: pointwise transcendence saturates

`CONJECTURED`: apply Gaudron's explicit algebraic-coefficient logarithmic-form
bound to the Cycle-97 simple-root form

```text
Lambda=D log(alpha)-2pi
      =D log(alpha)+2i log(-1).
```

For `k=Q(i,alpha)`, the field degree can be proportional to the sparse
trinomial degree `M`. The auxiliary integer and each of the two remaining
factors in Gaudron's bound then appear to cost one power of that degree,
suggesting a fourth-power degree loss. At `M=X^(3/5+o(1))`, this would give
only `exp(-X^(12/5+o(1)))`, not the required power of `X`.

This would saturate only the direct worst-case insertion of this theorem.
It would not exclude a sparse-polynomial refinement, a bounded-degree
subsector, an average over modes, or a critical-point count.

# Cycle 96 discovery candidate: integer jets control small projective modes

`CONJECTURED`: put

```text
f(x)=A-B exp(a x)-C exp(b x),
A,B,C positive integers, a,b integers, x=2pi/D.
```

For the projective entropy residual, `(A,B,C)=(p0 n,p0 n',q0m)` and
`(a,b)=(u,u+v)`.  At `x=0`, both

```text
J0=A-B-C,       J1=Ba+Cb
```

are integers.  This suggests a trichotomy on a sufficiently small mode
sector:

1. `J0!=0`: the constant integer gap survives the exponential perturbation;
2. `J0=0`, `J1!=0`: the linear integer gap survives while the derivative
   cannot turn;
3. `J0=J1=0`: strict concavity gives a quadratic gap controlled by
   `Ba^2+Cb^2`.

This is only a candidate until the sector hypotheses, signs, and constants
are frozen and proved.

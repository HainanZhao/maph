## Derivation A: exact lcm resonance (CONJECTURED until independently replayed)

Write the two orbit errors as

```text
epsilon_l=A-U*alpha_l,       epsilon_m=B-V*alpha_m,
U=v*u, V=v*w, g=gcd(u,w), L=lcm(U,V)=v*u*w/g.
```

The primitive determinant gives the exact identity

```text
F/g - L*(alpha_l-alpha_m)
 = (w/g)*epsilon_l-(u/g)*epsilon_m.                         (A1)
```

Thus the two individual orbit inequalities imply the coefficient-preserving
cross-label resonance

```text
||L*(alpha_l-alpha_m)||
 <= (w/g)*2C/((N_l-1)X)+(u/g)*2C/((N_m-1)X).                (A2)
```

This makes the proposed Bezout engine precise: its only new arithmetic datum
is the least-common-multiple resonance, equivalently the divisibility
`g|F`.  At this stage (A2) is not an in-box bound: it is algebraically
generated by the two retained approximations.  Any claimed saving must use
actual exponential label distribution for the varying `L`, not the
determinant identity by itself.

## Derivation B: scalable nonrational local prototype (CONJECTURED)

The following family is designed to falsify a purely integer-only
compression.  Let `T` be a sufficiently large odd integer and set

```text
X=T^50, H=T^22, V=T^11, U=V^2=T^22, n=T^30,
B=(3V+1)/2, q=B/V, M=T^80,
r=sqrt(q^2+1/M), z=r^(1/n), Delta=2*pi*n/log(r).
```

Then `alpha_n=r` and `alpha_(2n)=r^2=q^2+1/M` are values of one actual
positive exponential.  The first is nonrational: after multiplying `r^2`
by the square denominator `V^2 M`, its numerator is
`(B*T^29)^2+1`, strictly between consecutive squares after reduction.

With `beta=rho=0`, retain the full rows of the two rational lines

```text
j=(B/V)h              at label n,
j=(B^2/V^2)h          at label 2n,
```

whose heights lie in `[H,2H]`.  Their residuals are respectively bounded by
`2H/(M(r+q))` and `2H/M`, hence are below `1/X`.  The primitive data are

```text
(u,w,A,B)=(V,V^2,B,B^2),
F=w*A-u*B=V*B*(V-B) != 0,
r_label=n,
N_n=T^11+1, N_(2n)=2.
```

The physical product `n*V*V^2=T^63` is stable against the inherited
`H*Delta/X=Theta(T^2)` threshold; `2n/Delta=log(r)/pi<1/2` for large `T`, so
both labels lie in the inherited chart.  This preserves common intercept,
full fibres, distinct labels, physical gaps, nonzero determinant, and both
near-orbit widths while having a nonrational phase.

Both depths are below the critical `X^(6/25)=T^12` seed scale. Its one
ordered label-pair carries only
`binom(T^11+1,2)*binom(2,2)=Theta(T^22)=X^(11/25)` physical rectangles,
strictly below the `X^(21/25)` populated-box scale.  It is therefore a
local nonrational stress test, not a critical saturator.

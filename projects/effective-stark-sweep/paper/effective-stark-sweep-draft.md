# An effective census of unconditional archimedean Stark instances

## First new theorem: an order-six instance

For \(K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt7)\) and
\(\mathfrak m=\mathfrak p_7\infty_2\), the one-place and two-place ray
groups are \(C_6\) and \(C_6\times C_2\). The differenced invariant has
Fourier support of orders two and six. Both unit-congruence hypotheses
hold and
\[
 [H:H\cap\mathbb Q^{\rm ab}]=2.
\]

The commutator-fixed field yields two imaginary quadratic bases without
any base being guessed in advance. Independently reconstructing from
their intrinsic conductors gives the same degree-24 normal closure as
the full ray field over both \(\mathbb Q(i)\), of conductor \((7)\),
and \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})\), of conductor
\(\mathfrak p_2^2\bar{\mathfrak p}_2^2\mathfrak p_7\).
The shorter \(\mathbb Q(i)\) divisor table has two rows and safe
exponent \(4032\).

Within the frozen literature perimeter, this is the first
unconditional order-six archimedean Stark algebraicity instance. It
shows that character order six is not the dimension-six obstruction:
the obstruction there is the failure of the applicable
index-two/CM-descent and ramification structure.

The exact candidate packet is
\[
\begin{aligned}
P(X)={}&X^6-(6+2\sqrt7)X^5+(22+8\sqrt7)X^4\\
 &-(34+13\sqrt7)X^3+(22+8\sqrt7)X^2\\
 &-(6+2\sqrt7)X+1.
\end{aligned}
\]
Its absolute polynomial is irreducible, defines the correct ray field
over \(K\), has six Sturm-isolated positive roots, and has Frobenius
generators labeled by split primes 19 and 31.

For the ray class represented by \((3)^r\), the Yamamoto cone is
generated by
\[
 \lambda_r=(7-2\sqrt7)/3^r,\qquad
 \lambda_r(8+3\sqrt7).
\]
Its lattice has determinant nine in
\(\mathfrak p_7/(3)^r\), and exact enumeration gives nine points in
the half-open parallelotope. After homogeneity, each factor is a
double sine with periods \(1\) and \(8+3\sqrt7\). Forgetting the other
real place and applying Yamamoto's place relation gives exactly twice
the surviving real-place logarithm.

Arb enclosures of the 27 independent double sines identify all six
Sturm-isolated roots. With safe exponent 4032, the powered height upper
bound is \(9.20\times10^{-9}\); Voutier's minimum for degrees
\(3\) through \(24\) is \(5.22\times10^{-5}\), giving margin \(>5688\).
Degree one follows from positivity of a rational unit, and degree two
from the \(\frac12\log\phi\) height bound. Hence the displayed packet
identity is `VERIFIED`. The independent PARI comparison remains only a
`NUMERICAL` cross-check.

The replay bundle is case `RQ-000190` (the dedicated theorem record is
`data/q7-p7-case-v1.json`, SHA-256
`f5f68b12163f4a884e860a92ddd2dd0757c138bb9a6fb49ec3ccc780fb3030b7`);
the independent Arb certificate has SHA-256
`a727ecf67311ab6d1e63f25dca79dc2246e559545cbb14cde3ac967692df18c9`.
Thus order six is provable when the index-two hypothesis holds; the
dimension-6 obstruction is the index/wildness failure, not the
character order.

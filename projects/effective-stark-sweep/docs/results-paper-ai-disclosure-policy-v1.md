# Results-paper AI disclosure policy check

Recorded: 2026-07-30 UTC

This note records the submission-facing policy check for the current
candidate journal families. Policies can change, so the selected
journal's author instructions must be checked again immediately before
submission.

## Official sources checked

- Elsevier, “Generative AI policies for journals”:
  <https://www.elsevier.com/en-au/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals>
  requires a separate declaration immediately before the references,
  naming the tool, its purpose, the author's review, and the author's
  responsibility. Research-process use should also be described in the
  methods or equivalent account.
- Springer Nature, “AI guidance for our researchers and communities”:
  <https://group.springernature.com/gp/group/ai/ai-guidance-for-our-researchers-and-communities>
  requires human accountability, forbids AI authorship, and requires
  substantive generative-AI use to be declared in the introduction or
  acknowledgments; copy editing alone need not be declared.
- American Mathematical Society, “Use of Artificial Intelligence”:
  <https://www.ams.org/publications/journals/policies/UseofArtificialIntelligence>
  and the AMS/COPE policy record require authorship to remain human and
  AI use to be disclosed. The policy page returned HTTP 403 to the
  automated checker on 2026-07-30, so its text must be checked manually
  before an AMS submission.

## Manuscript decision

Use a separate, unnumbered section immediately before the bibliography:

> Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the
> manuscript preparation process

The declaration names OpenAI GPT-5.6 and Anthropic Claude, states their
uses, and states that the author reviewed and
verified every output, made every mathematical and editorial decision,
and accepts full responsibility. This placement directly follows the
Elsevier journal format and is at least as transparent as the
Springer/AMS disclosure baseline.

No AI system is named as an author or cited as mathematical authority.
The mathematical proof chain is presented in the body and the exact
certificate/provenance account in the appendix. The target journal's
current instructions remain the final authority at submission.

# Census-paper preregistration amendment v8: H taxonomy and frontier order

Frozen: 2026-07-31 UTC, before assembling the 2,704-row H taxonomy or
opening any frontier minimum.

The H universe is exactly the frozen W1 rows whose exact support-order
profile contains an order greater than two.  The support-first
reconciliation must reproduce 2,704 rows and the mutually exclusive
v5 routing counts \(232+881+1591\).

Every row will keep three logically separate fields:

1. exact packet resolution (selected results-paper theorem or open);
2. current Engine-B/Engine-C route eligibility;
3. Roblot weak-theorem coverage.

Route eligibility and Roblot coverage are not case-level packet
identifications.

Roblot full-row weak coverage is declared only if every higher-order
supported character is covered.  Thus every higher support order must
belong to \(\{4,6\}\); every order-four inverse-pair kernel must have a
complete A1--A3 screen with Theorem 6.1 eligible, and every order-six
kernel must have a complete six-gate Theorem 7.1 screen with the theorem
applicable.  A failed or capped kernel makes the row incomplete, never
negative by omission.

For each individual higher support order, “minimal” means the smallest
stable numeric RQ id.  Two tables are frozen:

- the minimal row not already identified by a selected results-paper
  theorem;
- the minimal such row for which Engine B, Engine C, and full-row
  Roblot weak coverage all fail.

A row supporting several higher orders may occur in several order
columns.  The \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{21})\) order-six wall is audited
separately rather than selected after seeing the minima.

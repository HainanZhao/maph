## Proposal corrections

1. Rational weights add \(\prod_j\mathrm{den}(\gamma_j)\) to the stated
   master-denominator multiple.
2. The integral-weight reference denominator at
   \(N=2^{20},d=100\) has 4,279 bits, not 4,743.
3. Denominator bits were insufficient to freeze a CRT schedule; the
   required signed numerator and difference bounds are now proved.
4. CBC candidates \(z\) and \(N-z\) are exact mirrors, so the proposed
   raw tie-rate expectation was ill-posed.
5. The unnamed “five siblings” were not inherited; a deterministic
   independently audited 16-prime schedule replaces them.
6. Claims about production use, universal binary64 practice, and
   under-an-hour performance remain open audits.

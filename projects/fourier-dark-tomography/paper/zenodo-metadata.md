## Title

Dimension-saturating local tomography of off-diagonal
Fourier-interferometer errors from displaced dark events

## Description / abstract

Dark output events are useful null tests for linear-optical devices, but
their unprobed probabilities have no signed linear response to a coherent
error. We append positive and negative calibrated output displacements and
use their count difference as a phase-sensitive local observable. We prove
that number-state inputs leave an exact \((m-1)\)-dimensional gauge at an
\(m\)-mode Fourier interferometer, whereas a phase-calibrated fixed-number
multimode cat removes that continuous obstruction. For every \(m\geq2\)
and every \(n>2\) divisible by \(m\), an explicit family of dark outcomes
and two fixed probes gives a full-rank local Jacobian for all \(m(m-1)\)
off-diagonal Hermitian error coordinates using four programmed settings.
The scalar response count saturates a dimension bound within the declared
weak-probe model. We also give exact four-mode certificates, a weighted
local estimator, finite-angle and finite-shot tests, and an augmented
analysis showing that unknown cat amplitudes as well as phases are
confounded with device errors. The construction is an identifiability
result, not a scalable replacement for conventional optical
characterization: at \(n=m\) its reference probabilities decay as
\(O(\epsilon^2m^{2-m})\) for unit-spectral-norm probes.

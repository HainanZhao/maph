## Objective and scope

<State the object, model, and motivating old phenomenon. Define the campaign's

<State what is not being claimed, especially any larger open problem.>

## Starting evidence

<List the relevant prior source or artifact and label its status: PROVED,
CERTIFIED_NUMERICAL, RECOGNIZED, OBSERVED, or CONJECTURED.>

## Workstreams

### Gate 1 — <mechanism>

<Question and proposed invariant or construction.>

**Acceptance:** <what establishes a result.>

**Kill/escalate:** <what refutes this mechanism, and where to record it.>

### Gate 2 — <independent route>

...

## Verification and reproducibility

<Exact proof/certification standard, independent audits, source and convention
requirements, and failure-ledger rule.>

## Public explanation or demo gate (optional)

<State exactly which result licenses a demo and how observations will be
labeled.>

## Stop conditions and terminal outcomes

<Say what closes a gate, what closes the campaign, and what does not count as
completion.>
